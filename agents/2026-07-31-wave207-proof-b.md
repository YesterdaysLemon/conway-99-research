## Verdict

The mixed tensor has an exact interpretation, but it does not by itself
exclude the endpoint.  Put

```text
F_yx=P_yP_xP_y,
M[(y,x),(v,z)]=tr(F_yxF_vz),
F=span_(y,x){F_yx}.
```

For any synthesis map `R` and its trace Gram `G`, there is a short exact
sequence

```text
0 -> ker(R) -> ker(G) -> rad(im R) -> 0.
```

Consequently

```text
ker(M)/ker(R_all) isomorphic to rad(F),
rank(R_all)-rank(M)=dim rad(F).
```

This identifies precisely what mixed centers repair: fixed-slice Gram-null
directions disappear unless they remain radical in the global sandwich
span.  It also prevents promotion of mixed-Gram nullity to a true operator
kernel without an independent coordinate-rank calculation.

In seven-star coordinates, with `C_yv=Z_y^*Z_v`, the mixed tensor is

```text
M[(y,x),(v,z)]
 =tr(R_x^(y) C_yv R_z^(v) C_vy).
```

If `d=rank(C_yv)`, the complete `21 by 21` cross-center transition has

```text
rank=binom(d+1,2).
```

The checker replays ranks `0,1,3,6,10,15,21` for `d=0,...,6`, including the
same-center determinant-two metric.

Four middle centers are the first that can span the ambient 66-dimensional
self-adjoint operator space, because three contribute at most 63 dimensions.
A rank-66 mixed Gram minor from four actual middle rows would prove joint
injectivity and eliminate every radical false kernel.  After a full-span
pair of six-spaces, this becomes a rank-25 system on one `5 by 5` cross
matrix.  An analytic irreducible-companion control attains rank 25 and
cumulative spans `21,41,56,66`, proving the certificate is algebraically
sharp but not that the endpoint satisfies it.

Finally, the star identity `BD=0` corrects a Wave 206 caveat.  For
`a=B^Tc`, one has `Da=0` and hence the true centered-column relation
`Za=0`.  If `a in A_Delta`, then `sum_x c_xP_x=0`, so

```text
sum_x c_xF_yx=0,
sum_x c_xM[(y,x),(v,z)]=0
```

for every `y,v,z`.  Mixed traces therefore cannot distinguish or exclude
the M7g/weight-eight relation once it is already a true projector relation;
the missing invariant is nonlinear or graph-typed data on its point coloring,
support triangles, and star placements.
