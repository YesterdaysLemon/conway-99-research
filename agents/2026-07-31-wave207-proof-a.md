## Result

The Wave 206 intersection code was

```text
A_Delta=im(B^T) intersect ker(a |-> D diag(a)D).
```

The incidence half of this definition supplies an additional exact relation:

```text
ZB^T=0,
so a in A_Delta implies sum_T a_T z_T=0.
```

This corrects the earlier scope note that treated the tensor word as not
necessarily a relation among the original centered columns.  That warning is
valid for an arbitrary tensor relation, but not after imposing
`a in im(B^T)`.

Together with the verified tensor equation, the correction completely
classifies the equality case.  If `wt(a)=8`, then:

```text
coefficient composition:             4 ones and 4 twos;
support span:                         vector rank 4;

                                      external point, no three coplanar;

internal circuits:                    12 projective weight-4 and
                                      8 projective weight-5 circuits;

```

The support classification has an elementary proof.  Maximal isotropy puts
the all-one coordinate vector in the support row space.  In an affine chart,
the positive and negative four-sets have equal moments through degree two.
Each is an affine tetrahedron.  After centering, the tetrahedral `S4` is an
index-two subgroup of `O(3,3)`; disjointness forces the negative tetrahedron
to be the central reflection of the positive one.

Restricting the endpoint polar form to this canonical support leaves only
27 forms.  Their exact rank/orthogonality types are

```text

rank 2: 12 forms, 2K4;
rank 3:  8 forms, 4K2;

```

At the prism-free endpoint, orthogonality of two distinct selected block
columns means that their graph triangles are disjoint and anticomplete.
Thus the four rows above are four necessary triangle-incidence patterns.

Kaipa--Pradhan's rank-seven Veronese classification independently contains
the same ambient `M_7g` orbit.  The incidence-linear coefficient theorem and
