## 3. Equality forces the equitable `27+72` partition

```text

```

reverse inequality.  Hence

```text

```

Minimum degree six gives `e(X)>=81`, while (4) at `m=27` gives
`e(X)<=81`.  Consequently `G[X]` is exactly 6-regular.

This is equality in the Rayleigh bound, not merely equality of two integer
counts.  If `x=1_X` and

```text
x=(27/99)1+y=(3/11)1+y,       y perpendicular to 1,
```

then equality implies `Ay=3y`.  Therefore

```text
Ax=3x+3*1.
```

Every vertex outside `X` has exactly three neighbors in `X`; every vertex in
`X` has eight neighbors outside.  The partition is equitable:

```text

from X    6     8
from Y    3    11.                                      (7)
```

## 4. Exact block algebra and the outside-neighborhood design

Write the adjacency matrix in the equitable ordering as

```text
A = [ C  N ]
    [N^T E ],
```

with shapes `27,27`, `27,72`, and `72,72`.  Equation (7) gives

```text
C1=6*1, N1=8*1, N^T1=3*1, E1=11*1.                     (8)
```

The full SRG identity is exactly equivalent to

```text
C^2+NN^T = 12I-C+2J,                                   (9)
CN+NE     = 2J-N,                                      (10)
N^TN+E^2  = 12I-E+2J.                                  (11)
```

In particular,

```text
NN^T=12I-C-C^2+2J.                                     (12)
```

Hence every nonprincipal eigenvalue of `C` lies in `[-4,3]`.  Every column
of `N` has weight three, every row has weight eight, and the 72 columns are
distinct: two equal columns would give two outside vertices at least three
common neighbors.

For points `p!=q` in `X`, put

```text
s_pq=2-C_pq-(C^2)_pq.                                  (13)
```

Then `s_pq` is the number of outside vertices whose `X`-neighborhood
contains `{p,q}`.  Thus (9) is a finite binary exact-cover problem:

```text
z_T in {0,1}                     for T in C(X,3),
sum_{T contains p} z_T = 8,
sum_{T contains p,q} z_T = s_pq.                        (14)
```

The selected triples are precisely the 72 outside `X`-neighborhoods.  This
is a useful exact first-stage feasibility encoding; it has no automorphism
assumption and a negative conclusion would require complete enumeration and
a checked certificate.

## 5. The active point graph and exact support identity

By (6), each point is a two-set of active labels.  Regard it as an edge of a
graph `F` on the eighteen active labels.  Linearity makes `F` simple, every
active label occurs in three points, and the common-point rule makes `F`
triangle-free.  Therefore

```text
F is a simple cubic triangle-free graph on 18 vertices and 27 edges.
```

Let `H` be its 18-by-27 vertex-edge incidence matrix.  The four
active-triangle neighbors of a point form the line graph

```text
D=L(F)=H^T H-2I.
```

The fixed sum at a `(2,2)` point gives exactly two positive-support
neighbors.  Since `G[X]` has no degree left over, those edges form a simple
2-factor `R` on the 27 point objects and

```text
C=D+R.                                                  (15)
```

An `R`-edge joins disjoint edges of `F`; its labeled crossing is a full
`K2,2` in `L`.  Every `L`-edge represents one `N3` side pair and is covered
by its two cross graph-edges.  Consequently the rectangle-coverage statement
is the exact integer matrix identity

```text
H R H^T=2L.                                             (16)
```

Here `R` and `L` denote adjacency matrices.  Equation (16) is a compact,
machine-encodable replacement for 54 separate twofold-coverage statements.

## 6. New mod-two support-cycle obstruction

Reducing (16) modulo two gives

```text
H R H^T=0 over F2.                                      (17)
```

Thus `U=row(H)` is a totally isotropic subspace for the alternating
bilinear form with matrix `R`.  If `c(F)` is the number of connected
components of `F`, the ordinary incidence-rank formula gives

```text
dim_F2 U=18-c(F).                                       (18)
```

If the 2-factor `R` has `c(R)` cycles, of which `e(R)` have even length,
then the adjacency matrix of an odd cycle has nullity one over `F2`, while
an even cycle has nullity two.  Hence

```text
nullity_F2(R)=c(R)+e(R).                                (19)
```

An alternating form on a 27-dimensional space with radical dimension
`nu` has maximal totally isotropic dimension `(27+nu)/2`.  Combining
(17)--(19) gives

```text
18-c(F) <= (27+c(R)+e(R))/2,
c(R)+e(R) >= 9-2c(F).                                  (20)
```

A simple cubic triangle-free component has at least six vertices, so
`c(F)<=3`.  Exact consequences are:

| `c(F)` | required `c(R)+e(R)` | minimum number of `R` cycles |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 7 | 4 |
| 2 | 5 | 3 |
| 3 | 3 | 2 |

In particular `R=C27` is impossible in every case.  The checker enumerates
all partitions of 27 into cycle lengths at least three and reproduces these
minimum component counts.  This is only necessary: many cycle partitions
survive.

Modulo two, (9) also gives

```text
NN^T=C+C^2.
```

No rank contradiction follows from this identity alone.  Over `F3`, the
equitable-set indicator satisfies `A1_X=0`, providing a ternary kernel word
of weight 27; this is compatible with the currently audited modular ranks.
