## Executive forecast

The literal one-edge pivot/mate modular-rank strategy has **zero probability
of producing an endpoint contradiction by rank-floor iteration alone**. This
is a structural impossibility, not pessimism: its matrix is only `39 x 39`,
so it cannot prove a local rank above `39`, while the endpoint global
characteristic-seven rank ceiling is `44`. A contradiction therefore needs
something outside the current strategy, such as a theorem that several
overlapping edge blocks contribute independent global directions.

The same strategy is nevertheless quite likely to produce another meaningful
theorem. On the current discovery evidence, I assign:

| Event | Subjective probability |
| --- | ---: |
| The current conditional endpoint `rank_F7(M)>=28` candidate survives genuinely independent verification | **80-95%** |
| The one-edge pivot/mate line yields at least one further useful local classification or rank theorem after rank 28 | **25-50%** |
| The one-edge rank-floor line, without a new cross-block/global bridge, strictly excludes `n3=4158` | **0%** |
| The rank work plus a genuinely new cross-block compatibility theorem strictly excludes the endpoint within one month of comparable effort | **5-15%** |

The first range is high because the even-type and type-`3+3` discovery
packages report complete exact searches with zero survivors. It is not
`100%` because both are still discovery-side, the even-type package was a
live incomplete package at this snapshot, and shared premises or a
completeness error can survive tests written by the discoverer.

## What “the current strategy” means

Here it means the Wave 41--43 method that:

1. chooses one graph edge and forms its `39`-vertex principal block;
2. decomposes its rank over `F_7` into a fixed scaffold, a border matrix `F`,
   and a symmetric residual `D`; and
3. uses invertible pivots, Schur complements, and mate/nonmate constraints to
   exhaust low-rank residuals.

This method is excellent at excluding low local ranks. It does not, by itself,
measure how the row or column spaces from different edges overlap inside the
same `99 x 99` global matrix. That overlap is exactly what must be controlled
to turn local rank progress into a contradiction with the global ceiling.

Even a perfect continuation to local rank `39` leaves the global interval
`39 <= rank_F7(M) <= 44` nonempty. Reaching rank `40` locally is impossible
for a `39 x 39` block. Thus no amount of faster enumeration repairs this
particular obstruction.

## Genuine-solution probabilities

“Solution” below means either a complete `99`-vertex graph independently
checked against every strongly regular condition, or a complete checked
nonexistence proof. A stricter upper bound, an endpoint exclusion, a rank
theorem, or a solver timeout is not counted.

The reference-class prior for a decades-resistant finite existence problem
being solved by one program in days is very low. There is no adequate data set
from which to infer a calibrated rate, so the following are deliberately
broad subjective bands.

| Comparable elapsed effort | Conservative assumptions | Liberal assumptions |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| one day | **0.05-0.2%** | **0.5-2%** |
| one week | **0.2-1%** | **3-8%** |
| one month | **1-3%** | **10-25%** |

These columns are conditional scenarios, not competing confidence intervals.

### Conservative assumptions

- Low-rank local witnesses begin to survive at the next few layers.
- No theorem controls independent rank contributed by overlapping edge
  blocks.
- The compact `B` model remains proof-hard, and adding `H` is substantially
  harder.
- The `264` lifts do not collapse to a few proved equivalence classes.
- Endpoint methods do not automatically generalize to the roughly
  `708 <= n3 <= 4158` global range.

Under these assumptions there is no honest finite ETA from the current
strategy. For planning purposes, a genuine solution is a **2-10+ year or
indefinite** research program, even though useful intermediate theorems can
continue to arrive daily.

### Liberal assumptions

- The conditional rank-28 theorem verifies with no repair beyond metadata.
- A cross-block packing lemma is found that converts local rank defects into
  new global dimensions.
- The compact matching model has a decisive hidden structure: either it emits
  one full `B`, or a manageable proof-producing UNSAT trace.
- The outside graph `H` is then forced or quickly contradictory.
- For a negative solution, the resulting invariant globalizes beyond the
  all-`222`, `r3=12`, rank-33 branch; alternatively, a positive `B/H`
  completion directly yields a full graph.

Under all of those favorable assumptions, a genuine solution has a plausible
wall-clock range of **one to six months**. A positive construction could land
in **two to twelve weeks** because one checked graph settles existence. A
complete nonexistence proof is asymmetric and likely slower: even in the
liberal scenario, **six to twenty-four months** is more credible unless a
single global invariant collapses all branches at once.

## Dominant failure modes

1. **Dimension ceiling.** One local block cannot outrun the global rank
   ceiling.
2. **Rank witnesses appear.** Rank-two residuals were eliminated, but
   rank-three and higher residuals have much larger pivot state spaces and
   may actually exist.
3. **Local-to-global overlap.** Strong rank in every block need not add; the
   same global subspace can support many local blocks.
4. **Incidence completion hardness.** Tens of thousands of individually legal
   columns must satisfy exact simultaneous concurrence equations.
5. **Outside-graph compatibility.** A legal `B` is not yet a legal `H`, much
   less a complete graph.
6. **Scope fragmentation.** Results conditioned on the endpoint, `r3=12`,
   all edges type `222`, or one lift do not cover the full problem.
7. **Certificate scale.** A solver can find a terminal result that is still
   unusable if its proof is too large, not retained, or not independently
   checkable.
8. **Correlated verification risk.** Separate programs can still share an
   incorrect mathematical reduction or frozen-input premise.

## Evidence that would materially update the forecast

Strong upward updates:

- an independent clean-room verification of conditional endpoint rank `28`;
- a theorem giving a quantitative lower bound for the dimension added by two
  or more overlapping edge blocks;
- one complete sixty-column `B` certificate or one retained and independently
  checked UNSAT proof for the compact model;
- a proof that the `264` lifts reduce to a small number of equivalent exact
  completion instances;
- reconstruction or contradiction of `H` from a certified `B`;
- an invariant that applies away from `n3=4158`, rather than only to the
  prism-free endpoint.

Strong downward updates:

- explicit rank-three residual witnesses across every endpoint local type;
- many certified `B` solutions with large families of partially compatible
  `H`, showing the present equations are loose;
- proof-producing runs whose traces grow beyond practical checking limits;
- further complete cut families that add no units, implications, or branch
  closures;
- a verified counterexample to the shared local-to-global rank transport or
  completeness premises.

The central qualitative judgment is therefore: **the modular pivot/mate lane
smells productive but not terminal**. It is well positioned to deliver
rank `28` and better normal forms. The breakthrough needed for “finish him”
is a cross-block/global compatibility theorem or a certified `B/H`
completion, not another isolated increment of the one-edge rank floor.
