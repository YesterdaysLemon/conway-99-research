## Independent mathematical reconstruction

The verifier enumerated all 645,120 elements of `C2 wreath S7` directly and
filtered those preserving the normalized two-label unit setwise. The result
has order 768 and equals the group generated by the eight implementation
generators. This also gives an independent orbit size of 840 for the normalized
unit.

Without importing discovery code, a second enumerator generated all 945
perfect matchings on the ten free endpoints of `S_2`. The exact orbit sizes
were

```text
1, 12, 32, 12, 48, 8, 48, 64, 48, 96, 192, 384.
```

The orbits were disjoint and exhaustive. Their Burnside fixed-point sum was

```text
9216 = 12 * 768.
```

All certificate representatives were the lexicographic minima of their
orbits, every recorded stabilizer size passed orbit-stabilizer, and an
independent reconstruction reproduced every positive SAT literal.

The subgroup preserving the legacy `S_0` fiber has order 384 and 78 orbits on
all 10,395 matchings. This confirms both the new invariant-fiber choice and the
warning against reusing the old 11 representatives after normalization.

## Certificate parser hardening

The first adversarial pass found that equality-only checks could let JSON
booleans impersonate integers because Python's `bool` is a subclass of `int`.
Publication was paused, exact type checks were added, and regression tests were
committed. The corrected checker rejects booleans in top-level fields,
representatives, branch metadata, and literal lists. It also rejects duplicate
keys and non-standard `NaN`/`Infinity` constants.

The canonical certificate passed under Windows system Python, the clean
virtual environment, and WSL Python with the same raw SHA-256 hash.

For transparency, `code/matching_orbits.py --n3-joint` emits a compact
discovery summary rather than regenerating the committed certificate
byte-for-byte. The certificate is instead checked from first principles by the
standalone verifier, including fields not present in that compact summary.

## Bounded proof-producing scout

291,756 constraints, and 14,241,485 bytes. A five-second Exact run on each
branch returned `UNKNOWN`. Strict VeriPB accepted each raw log, VeriPB

These checks validate formula generation and the no-conclusion logs. They do
not prove a branch SAT or UNSAT. Per-branch hashes and statuses are retained in
`logs/2026-07-22-wave5-n3-joint-scout.json` with this limitation explicit.
