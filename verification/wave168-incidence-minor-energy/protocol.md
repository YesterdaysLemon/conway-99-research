# Independent verification protocol

The verifier reconstructed the incidence geometry without trusting the
discovery arithmetic.

Checks:

1. distinguish unmarked triangle blocks from edge-marked copies;
2. derive all spectra, ranks, and zero multiplicities;
3. prove the cross-edge/common-block bijection and both prism directions;
4. audit the cube and Mobius-ladder eigenvalues and determinants;
5. reproduce the global Cauchy-Binet coefficient identity;
6. use the exact rectangular definition of the neighborhood-triple matrix;
7. check its diagonal, adjacent, and disjoint row intersections; and
8. restrict modular conclusions to Gram-level facts.

No computation was permitted below the host memory floor. No literature
novelty or endpoint conclusion can be promoted from these identities alone.
