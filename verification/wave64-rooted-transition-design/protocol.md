2. Import no discovery module and trust no solver status.
3. Reconstruct `K14 - 7K2`, its 84 edge labels, all 35,560 three-edge
   matchings, all 840 allowed transitions, all local perfect matchings, and
   all 280 transition-triangle cuts directly from definitions.
4. Treat numeric indices in the candidate as claims: rebuild the canonical
   ordering and compare every stored label, type, and support field.
5. Check every block-master row and every rational stronger-linear-master row
   with integers or `fractions.Fraction`.
6. Audit the residual-codegree formula against every rooted pair class and
   state its exact equivalence boundary.
7. Verify input and package manifests, run-report schema, mutation rejection,
   and byte-for-byte deterministic replay.
8. Keep endpoint, Conway-99, and novelty status `UNKNOWN` without an
   independently checked complete graph or proof.
