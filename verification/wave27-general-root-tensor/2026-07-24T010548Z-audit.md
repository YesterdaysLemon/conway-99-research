## 2. Frame transport, compression, and arbitrary cross-Q

For an integral congruence displaying

```text

```

```text

```

column rank, `X^T 1=0`; hence `sum_i z_i=0`. A concrete 21-row `A2` control

Writing `Q=X^T(M o M)X`, direct ordered-tensor expansion gives

```text
tr(R Q_RR)
```

The independent synthetic control has nonzero cross block
`Q_RC=(-7,-5)^T`; nevertheless its exact trace split is
`146=90+56`, its first-leg tensor norm is 90, and its pure `RRR` norm is 18.
Thus arbitrary cross-`Q` entries neither lower the compression nor enter the
trace split.

## 4. Divisibility and component contradictions

For integral `k`, `k^3=k (mod 6)`. Therefore

```text
||P_R||^2
 = sum_(i,j) <z_i,z_j>_R^3
 = ||sum_i z_i||_R^2
 = 0                                           (mod 6).
```

Dropping zero sum is active: the one-vector norm-two control has cubic energy
8, which is 2 modulo 6. Combining the empty balls with the actual zero sum
gives safe floors

```text
E6 >=24 >22,
A6 >=66 >60.
```

Hence both orthogonal summands are impossible. The proof does not claim that
24 or 66 is attained.

`det(Q)=3`, and `det(E6 Q)=9`. It correctly refutes the tempting algebra-only
trace floor while leaving the stronger cubic-origin theorem intact.

## 6. Later A20 addendum

The independently checked rank-one identity is

```text

      + sum_(i=1)^(n-1) (e_i-e_(i+1))(e_i-e_(i+1))^T,
```

results above, a full orthogonal ADE rank-44 form is conditionally impossible:
only `E8` remains locally, and 44 is not divisible by 8.
