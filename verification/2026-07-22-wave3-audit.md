
exactly and checked both directions of the PSD formulation. It separately
reconstructed 2,695 edge variables, 1,694 linear equations, 3,542 admissible

obstruction. The audit also corrected two phrases: the 77 lines are triples of
pairwise-disjoint blocks, not 77 mutually vertex-disjoint triples, and the
reported model is only the smallest clean formulation found in this lane.
