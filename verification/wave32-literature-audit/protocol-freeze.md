## 4. Rooted branch

The rooted branch means that some `r in Z^44` has

```text

```

The verified Wave 28 consequences, with no orthogonal-summand assumption,
are:

```text

div(r)=1,
K_r=r^perp intersect L,
[L:Zr orthogonal_sum K_r]=2,
det(K_r)=2h.
```

The index-two glue is essential: a primitive root need not be an integral
orthogonal `A1` summand.

Define its marked-frame image

```text

```

Then

```text
t_i in {0,+1,-1,+2,-2},
sum_i t_i=0,
t.t=42,
t(r).t(s)=21(r,s)_S,
Mt=21t.
```

coordinate-count patterns for one root. It does not construct a vector or
exclude roots.

At the actual-incidence layer there are the additional equivalent
conditions

```text

Nt in ker(A+4I),
||Nt||^2=126.
```

Let `R` be generated by all roots, `Rbar=(R tensor Q) intersect L`, and
`K=Rbar^perp intersect L`. The verified reduction gives

```text

K is rootless,
```

This does not make `R`, `Rbar`, or any single root an integral orthogonal

## 5. Rootless integrally indecomposable branch

This branch has

```text
min(S)>=4
```

and admits no nontrivial integral orthogonal decomposition. It is not
restricted to an indecomposable root lattice, a modular genus, a strongly
21-modular lattice, or a catalogue family.

The weak containment

```text

```

implies that the 3-primary and 7-primary discriminant components are
elementary and that the exact level is `3`, `7`, or `21`. It does not imply
that `L` is 21-modular or strongly 21-modular, because no similarity
`L* -> L` has been supplied.

Wave 31 proves that an actual endpoint cannot produce a proper coordinate
block of `E` from a rootless integral split. In this branch there is no split
from which to manufacture the diagonal sign involution. The remaining exact
problem is therefore an unrestricted marked-frame/Schur/incidence
realization or a new obstruction that does not presuppose a proper lattice or
coordinate block.
