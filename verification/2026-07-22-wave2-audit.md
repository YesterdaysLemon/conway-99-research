## Algebra checks

The algebra verifier constructed `C`, `M`, and all fixed products directly from
the lexicographic residual labels. Exact row reduction reproduced the claimed
characteristic-two and characteristic-three ranks. A separate primary-space
argument reproduced both residual Jordan forms.

For the full adjacency matrix, the verifier used

```text
84 A^(-1) = 7A + 7I - J
```

to bound every Smith factor by 84, then combined exact ranks modulo 2, 3, and 7
with the determinant. This avoids an unjustified integral eigenspace split.

For the residual kernel lattice, exact minors prove saturation index two. The
compound-matrix/pseudodeterminant identity then proves the top determinantal
divisor. The verifier explicitly rejected any inference fixing the individual
2-adic exponents from only their count and total valuation.
