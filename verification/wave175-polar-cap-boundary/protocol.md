# Verification protocol

1. Treat every Wave 175 statement as untrusted.
2. Verify all frozen hashes before reading mathematical claims.
3. Reconstruct the parabolic-quadric point count, collinearity degree,
   intersection parameters, and eigenvalues from exact formulas.
4. Recompute the selected-set spectral decomposition and outside-point first
   two moments with rational arithmetic.
5. Derive `b_z=0 mod 3` from the zero-frame identity and evaluate the
   consecutive allowed-value polynomial exactly.
6. Check the symmetric-square dimension, singular hyperplane, Schur-square
   identity, and both rank consequences.
7. Inspect the primary source's definition of a quadric cap; do not infer
   applicability from the paper title or abstract.
8. Execute the archived exact verification and focused tests.
9. Veto any claim of endpoint exclusion, improved `n3` bound, external
   novelty, or Conway-99 resolution.
