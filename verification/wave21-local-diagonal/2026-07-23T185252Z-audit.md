## 2. Tensor Gram diagonal

Factor the audited positive semidefinite rank-44 matrix as

```text
M[T,U]=<u_T,u_U>,  ||u_T||^2=4.
```

With

```text
S_T=sum_U M[T,U] u_U u_U^T,
```

directly expanding both ordered indices gives

```text
<S_T,S_U>
 =sum_(V,W) M[T,V] M[V,W]^2 M[W,U]
 =(M(M o M)M)[T,U]
 =A4[T,U].
```

Thus `A4[T,T]=||S_T||_F^2`.  No factor of two occurs: `V,W` are the two
ordered matrix-contraction indices, not an unordered combinatorial pair.
The independent toy-vector test checks all nine entries of this identity.

Since all frame vectors have squared norm four and `M1=0`,
`tr(S_T)=0`.  For

```text
P_T=u_T u_T^T-I/11
```

the exact traceless norm is

```text
||P_T||_F^2
 =16-2*(4/11)+(44/121)
 =172/11.
```

The identity component pairs to zero with `S_T`, while the remaining
contraction is the complete ordered Schur-cube row sum:

```text
<S_T,P_T>
 =sum_U M[T,U]^3
 =4^3+(20+q)-3q-8(12-q)
 =6(q-2).
```

Cauchy--Schwarz therefore gives

```text
A4[T,T] >= 36(q-2)^2/(172/11)
         = (99/43)(q-2)^2.
```

At the endpoint `tr(A4)=1008`.  Every other one of the 230 diagonals is a
positive multiple of four, so any selected diagonal is at most
`1008-230*4=88`.  The rational lower bound is at most 88 exactly for
`q=0,1,...,8`; it is `4851/43>88` at `q=9`.  Hence `q<=8`.

The independent rounded lower bounds in units of four are

```text
q:                0  1  2  3  4  5   6   7   8
A4[T,T]/4 at least:
                  3  1  1  1  3  6  10  15  21.
```

In particular, `q=1` is allowed by this package.  It was not silently
deleted.

## 3. Harmonic cubic and centered minor

For squared radius four in dimension 44, trace-removing a symmetric cubic
tensor gives the exact kernel

```text
<T(u),T(v)>
 =<u,v>^3 - (3*4^2/(44+2))<u,v>
 =<u,v>^3-(24/23)<u,v>.
```

This is a Gram construction.  It justifies positivity of

```text
H=23(M o M o M)-24M
```

even though a generic difference of two positive semidefinite matrices
need not be positive semidefinite.  The exact endpoint constants independently
reconstruct as

```text
H[T,T]=23*4^3-24*4=1376,
(H1)_T=23*6(q(T)-2)=138(q(T)-2),
1^T H 1=23*4*Delta=1104.
```

PSD Cauchy permits `q=10` and excludes `q=11`, so the one-vector harmonic
cutoff is exactly `q<=10`.

Let `r=H1` and `R=H-rr^T/1104`.  Factoring `H=BB^T` shows that `R` is the
Gram matrix after orthogonally projecting away `B^T1`; hence `R` is PSD.
For `q(T)=10`,

```text
R[T,T]=1376-1104=272.
```

For two distinct `q=10` indices, the four possible values
`M[T,U]=1,0,-1,-2` give centered entries

```text
-1105, -1104, -1103, -1240.
```

Every absolute value exceeds 272, so every corresponding `2`-by-`2`
determinant is negative.  The submitted multiplicity-at-most-one conclusion
passes.  As an extra hostile check, the independent checker evaluates every
`q(U)=0,...,10` and every off-diagonal `M` value: if a `q=10` index existed,
all other indices would in fact have `q<=5`.  This stronger observation is
still superseded here because the tensor budget already excludes `q=10`.

## 4. Trace square, cyclicity, and parity

Put `A=A4=MWM`, where `W=M o M`.  Two uses of `M^2=21M` are required:

```text
tr(A^2)
 =tr(M W M M W M)
 =21 tr(M W M W M)
 =21 tr(M^2 W M W)
 =441 tr(M W M W).
```

The last trace is an integer.  It is nonnegative because it is the squared
Frobenius norm of the symmetric positive semidefinite sandwich
`M^(1/2) W M^(1/2)`.  Thus the submitted
`t=tr(MWMW)` is a nonnegative integer.  An exact toy example with
`M^2=2M` distinguishes the correct square factor `2^2` from a hostile
single factor.

At `n3=705`, the number of ordered odd off-diagonal entries of `M` is

```text
sum_T (a_0(T)+a_2(T))
 =231*20+4*sum_T q(T)
 =4620+4*470
 =6500.
```

Symmetry and the even diagonal make this 3250 unordered positions.  Since
`A congruent M (mod 2)`, each unordered odd position contributes two
modulo eight to `tr(A^2)`.  Diagonal multiples of four and doubled squares
of even off-diagonal entries contribute zero modulo eight.  Therefore

```text
tr(A^2)=2*3250=4 (mod 8).
```

Treating 6500 as an unordered count would instead give zero modulo eight;
the hostile test rejects that factor mutation.  Because `441=1 (mod 8)`,
`t=4 (mod 8)`.

Finally, `rank(A)<=44`, `A` is PSD, and `tr(A)=1008`, so

```text
441t=tr(A^2)>=tr(A)^2/44,
t>=576/11.
```

The raw integer lower bound is 53.  The first integer at least 53 congruent
to four modulo eight is 60, giving the verified bound
`tr(A^2)>=441*60=26460`.

## 6. Spectral witness boundary

The submitted spectrum consists of 22 copies of each

```text

lambda_- = (252-21 sqrt(21))/11.
```

Both are positive.  Using symmetric sums avoids floating point:

```text

22(lambda_+^2+lambda_-^2)
 =44*((252/11)^2+(21/11)^2*21)

```

It therefore verifies feasibility of the nonnegative rank-44 first-two-
spectral-moment relaxation at the boundary `t=60`.  It does not provide
integral entries, prescribed diagonals, the parity pattern `A congruent M`,
the identity `AM=21A`, compatibility with the separate scalar profile, an
`M` or `W`, or a graph.  The submitted report states this boundary
correctly and does not promote the spectrum to a matrix certificate.
