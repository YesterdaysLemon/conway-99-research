## 3. Equality in the trace-square floor

Let `r` be the number of nonzero eigenvalues of `C`.  The nonzero
characteristic coefficient is a nonzero integer, so the absolute
pseudodeterminant is at least one.  AM-GM on eigenvalue squares and Cauchy on
their sum give

```text
tr(C^2) >= r,
tr(C^2) >= 64/r.
```

Exact enumeration for `1<=r<=44` has unique minimum eight at `r=8`.  If
`tr(C^2)=8`, equality in Cauchy with sum eight forces all eight nonzero
eigenvalues to equal one.  The remaining 36 eigenvalues are zero.  Real
diagonalizability then gives

```text
C^2=C,
spectrum(C)={1^8,0^36}.
```

Because `C` is an integral idempotent,

```text
Z^44=im(C) direct_sum ker(C)
```

as free abelian groups of ranks eight and 36.  This is stronger than a
rational eigenspace decomposition: it supplies a unimodular adapted basis.

## 4. The kernel-only scaled-dual obstruction

Choose a unimodular basis-change matrix `P` adapted to the integral split.
Then

```text

B'=diag(3I_8,I_36).
```

Self-adjointness gives `B'^T G'=G'B'`, where `G'=P^TGP`.  Comparing the
off-diagonal blocks with the distinct eigenvalues three and one shows that
`G'` is block diagonal:

```text

```

The transformed Gram matrix `Q'=P^TQP` is even integral, and

```text

Q0=G0/21.
```

The scaled dual transforms contragrediently:

```text

```

Since `P` is unimodular, this congruence preserves even integrality.  Thus
the kernel block is

```text

S0 Q0=I_36.
```

Both `S0` and `Q0` are even integral positive-definite Gram matrices, and
they are exact inverses.  Hence `Q0` is an even positive-definite unimodular
form of rank and signature 36.  The frozen even-unimodular signature theorem
requires its signature to be divisible by eight, but

```text

```

This contradiction excludes `tr(C^2)=8`.

The placement of the inverse identity is essential.  Globally,

```text

```

not `I`; therefore the global assertion `S=Q^(-1)` is false.  It becomes
valid only on the kernel block because `B0=I`.  Both the synthetic equality
control and the exact Wave 24 survivor are tested against this distinction.
