# Qualifying sources

Only primary or authoritative technical sources are used as evidence.
Secondary pages encountered in the result sets were treated as leads only.

## Conway-99

### S01 — Ibrahim, LaFayette, and McCall (2025)

- Type: peer-reviewed primary paper.
- Classification: **EXACT** for the statement that existence of an
  `srg(99,14,1,2)` is unknown; otherwise CONCEPTUAL/conditional.
- Evidence used: page 84 explicitly says existence is unknown and then
  derives conditional percolation consequences.
- URL:
  https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/93/ajc_v93_p060.pdf

### S02 — Keramatipour (2026)

- Type: primary arXiv preprint, v2 dated 2026-04-28.
- Classification: **NEAR-DIRECT** current-status evidence.
- Evidence used: treats Conway-99 as an existence problem, develops a SAT
  encoding, and reports that tested solvers cannot handle it in reasonable
  time; it does not claim a construction or impossibility proof.
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23037

### S03 — Reimbayev (2024)

- Type: primary paper/preprint.
- Classification: **EXACT** for its explicit unresolved-status statement;
  NEAR-DIRECT for structural hexagon bounds.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.62780/ejaam/2024-001
- Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10620

### S04 — Cesarz and Woldar (2025)

- Type: peer-reviewed primary paper.
- Classification: **EXACT** for its 2025 open-status statement;
  **NEAR-DIRECT** for its mathematics.
- Evidence used: the publisher abstract states that existence remains open,
  then the paper proves automorphism restrictions for a *putative* Conway
  99-graph; it is not a construction or general nonexistence result.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.5802/alco.418

### S05 — Crnkovic and Maksimovic (2020)

- Type: peer-reviewed primary paper.
- Classification: **NEAR-DIRECT**.
- Evidence used: explicitly says general existence is unknown and excludes
  selected automorphism groups of orders six and nine only.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.11575/cdm.v15i1.62323
- PDF: https://cdm.ucalgary.ca/article/download/62323/54015/204856

### S06 — Brouwer SRG parameter table

- Type: authoritative expert-maintained reference table.
- Classification: **EXACT** for the table's status notation.
- Evidence used: row `(99,14,1,2)` is marked `?`; live page last-modified
  header observed 2025-01-27.
- URL: https://aeb.win.tue.nl/graphs/srg/srgtab51-100.html

## Explicitly rejected candidate mismatch

Buckley and Waldron, “Quaternionic MUBs in `H^2` and their reflection
symmetries” (2026), DOI `10.13001/ela.2026.9905`, was exposed by O05.
Its abstract concerns quaternionic reflection groups, MUBs, and a
16-line quaternionic spherical design. It is not about the rank-six `E6`
root lattice and is not evidence for Themes B--D.
