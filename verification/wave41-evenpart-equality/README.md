## Even-part equality obstruction

For a triangle with twelve-point fibres `X,Y,Z`, let `P,Q,R` be their
internal perfect-matching matrices and normalize both `X-Y` and `X-Z` to the
identity. Let `F` be the `Y-Z` permutation matrix. Exact elimination in the
cubic neighbor-core Laplacian gives

```text

H = [ P+Q        F+3I+P ]
    [ F^T+3I+P   P+R   ].
```

For an edge type with `e` even parts, `A=P+Q` has nullity `2e`. Put
`B=F+3I+P`, let `N` span `ker(A)`, and let `W` span the right kernel of
`N^T B`. The Wave 40 minimum-projection boundary is `rank(N^T B)=e`.
Singular Schur elimination then gives rank 25 exactly when

```text
W^T R W = W^T (B^T A^- B-P) W.                  (1)
```

The verifier independently derives the equivalent 27-plus-12 formulation.
For `S=K[L,L]`, border `U`, `H0=ker(S)`, and `C=H0^T U`, let the columns of
`T` span `ker(C)` and solve `S X=U T`. Then

```text
rank(K39)=25
iff
T^T(W_Z T-U^T X)=0.
```

Both zero conditions are invariant under changes of kernel basis and
particular solution.
