# Wave 71 clean-room verification protocol

1. Freeze every discovery path, byte length, and SHA-256 before inspection.
2. Import only the independently verified Wave 66 theorem.
3. Reconstruct the index-three overlattice, its 7-dual, and Skoruppa's
   elementary-divisor weight without executing discovery code.
4. Compute the level-one reductions over `F_7` from the integral
   `E4^a E6^b` bases.
5. Enumerate every low-norm magnitude and sign profile, then exhaust every
   aggregate signed edge-count matrix subject to the exact eigenvector
   equations and the SRG spectral edge bound.
6. Use `lambda=1` and `mu=2` independently to refine the surviving signed
   supports.
7. Inspect and replay discovery only after the independent result exists.
8. Preserve `UNKNOWN` for graph existence, Conway-99, and literature novelty.

The verifier does not promote a discovery to an unconditional theorem and
does not edit the discovery package or any central index.
