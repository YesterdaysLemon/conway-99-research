## 1. Incidence and coloring reconstruction

Independently, over `F_3`,

```text
K=B^T*B,
K^2=B^T*(A+I)*B,
D=2(K-K^2)=B^T*A*B.
```

Wave 172 reduces a hypothetical weight-three word to three equal
coefficients supported on three triangle blocks inducing `3K3`.  For their
nine-point union `U`, the dependence gives

```text
B^T*A*1_U=0.
```

Writing `h=A1_U` and taking `c` to be the number of outside points with
`h=3`, the exact boundary and pair counts give

```text
(n0,n1,n2,n3)=(36-c,3c,54-3c,c).
```

The line-sum coloring has only block types `000,111,222,012`.  Reconstructing
the five-cell degree and `A^2=12I-A+2J` equations reproduces every entry of
the discovery edge matrix.  Pointwise equality of the two cross-color
degrees then gives

```text
t=72-3c.
```

No equitability or automorphism assumption enters.

## 2. Spectral audit and the repaired c=18 case

For `8<=c<=17`, the two discovery vectors are orthogonal to the invariant
space generated by `1_U`.  The independent Gram calculation gives

```text
G22  = 648(c-18)(c-12),
detG = -3359232*c(c-12)(c-9)(c-18)^3.
```

This excludes `c=10,11`; the negative first diagonal excludes `c=12`; and
the negative second diagonal excludes `13<=c<=17`.

The original draft incorrectly said nonnegativity stopped at 17.
Nonnegativity also permits `c=18`, where `T2` is empty and the displayed
two-vector basis degenerates.  On `(U,Z0,O1,T2,Z3)`, use instead

```text
v=(0,2,-1,0,1).
```

It is orthogonal to `1`, and exact substitution gives

```text
v^T*v=144,
v^T*A*v=1584,
v^T*(3I-A)*v=-1152.
```

This contradicts positive semidefiniteness of `3I-A` on `1^perp`.  The
repaired package now includes this case.  Only `c=8,9` remain.

## 3. Convexity and equality cases

For a point set `S`, the SRG common-neighbor identity is

```text
sum_x binomial(d_S(x),2)=2*binomial(|S|,2)-e(S).
```

At `c=8`, taking `S=Z3` gives cellwise convex lower bound

```text
21+20+0+0+8=49,
```

but the exact right side is 48.  Thus `c=8` is impossible.

At `c=9`, both sides equal 63.  Equality forces the pointwise `Z3` degrees

```text
(3,2,1,0,2)
```

on `(U,Z0,O1,T2,Z3)`, so `G[Z3]` is 2-regular.

Label a `Z3` point by its neighbor in each original `U` triangle.  Around a
`Z3` cycle, the two neighboring labels must be the two values different
from the center label; otherwise an adjacent pair violates `lambda=1` or a
nonadjacent pair has three common neighbors, violating `mu=2`.  Hence every
coordinate label has period three.

A cycle of length six or nine would contain two vertices with the same
three `U` neighbors and therefore at least three common neighbors.  Thus

```text
G[Z3]=3K3.
```

Each resulting `Z3` triangle has a perfect matching to each original
triangle.  This is a triangular prism, contrary to `P=0`.
