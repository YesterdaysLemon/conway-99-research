## What this does not prove

This verifies a necessary aggregate count system. It does not enforce that the
208 counts arise from overlapping induced subgraphs of one 99-vertex graph.
It supplies no graph construction and no evidence that `n3=4158` is attainable.
It does not improve the upper bound and does not resolve Conway-99.
