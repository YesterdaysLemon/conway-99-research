Role: independent verifier.

## Frozen target

Audit only the necessary discriminant and rootlessness consequences asserted
by `attempts/wave128-alternative-spaces`.  The target remains conditional on
a hypothetical `srg(99,14,1,2)` containing the frozen Wave 105 induced
`C4 Cartesian K3` motif.

## Independence

- Preinspect the discovery seal and imported manifest hashes before execution.
- Do not import or call the discovery checker from the verifier.
- Rebuild the incidence data and block action from the motif equations.
- Use integer Smith reduction, not the discovery DVR implementation.
- Evaluate Gauss sums with exact rational and cyclotomic arithmetic.
- Treat the seven-adic maps as a proof obligation, not as computed examples.
- Attack both signs in the norm-two argument.

## Status wall

The verifier may certify only the scoped necessary conditions.  It must keep
rank exclusion, motif extension, Conway-99, construction, and novelty
`UNKNOWN` unless separate evidence proves them.
