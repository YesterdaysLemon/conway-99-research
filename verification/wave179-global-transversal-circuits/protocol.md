# Verification protocol

1. Treat Wave 179 and its executable arithmetic as untrusted.
2. Validate every frozen input and package entry.
3. Extract a nonedge cross circuit without assuming an arbitrary minimal
   dependent subset is cross.
4. Require exact two-transversality and occurrence of both endpoints.
5. Prove the intersecting-family star/triangle dichotomy.
6. Audit disjoint realizations, including adjacent and diagonal repetitions
   among third vertices.
7. Use Wave 178 side balance to prove edge circuits have multiplicity one
   among all realizing pairs.
8. Count edge and nonedge projective classes separately before applying the
   two ternary scalar representatives.
9. Perform no graph, code, SAT, configuration, or isomorphism search.
10. Preserve the endpoint and global-existence boundary as `UNKNOWN`.
