# Failed routes and retained scope walls

## A fixed-slice Gram kernel is not a compression relation

For the synthesis

```text
R_y(c)=sum_x c_xP_yP_xP_y,
```

the exact quotient is

```text
ker(Tau^(y))/ker(R_y) isomorphic to rad(F_y).
```

In characteristic three the trace-zero self-adjoint operators on a
six-space have radical `<I_6>`.  The checker supplies 20 independent true
features whose trace Gram has rank 19.  The extra Gram nullity is not an
operator relation.

## `Phi(A)=0` is not the simultaneous-compression kernel

Although

```text
C^*C(A)=Phi(A)=sum_y P_yAP_y,
```

finite-field trace pairings are not positive definite.  `Phi(A)=0` says
that `C(A)` lies in the radical of `im(C)`; it need not say `C(A)=0`.
Any use of `ker Phi` as `ker C` requires a separate radical proof.

## Mixed-Gram nullity is also not a true feature relation

For the global sandwich synthesis `R_all`,

```text
ker(M)/ker(R_all) isomorphic to rad(F).
```

The mixed tensor removes slice-local radical directions only when they are
not radical in the global feature span.  A null word of `M` must not be
promoted unless the true operator-coordinate rank of `R_all` is also known.

## A full-rank cross transition is not enough by itself

If `rank(C_yv)=6`, the complete pairing between `Self(E_y)` and
`Self(E_v)` has rank 21.  The actual center features at `v` may nevertheless
span a proper or degenerate subspace of `Self(E_v)`.  Transition rank 21
does not imply slice feature rank 21 and does not prove that a particular
compression is detected.

## Three middle centers cannot certify ambient injectivity

Each middle row lies in a 21-dimensional local self-adjoint space.  Three
rows span at most 63 dimensions, below `dim Self(V)=66`.  No rearrangement
of three-center trace data can produce a full simultaneous-compression
certificate without extra nonlocal features.

## The four-center control is not endpoint evidence

The exact control with symmetric-square ranks `21,41,56,66` proves only that
the 25-coordinate certificate can be nonsingular.  It has four abstract
six-spaces in a square 11-space and lacks:

- the nonsquare endpoint ambient type;
- 99 star labels and the zero global frame;
- seven singular columns per star;
- 231 shared projectively distinct columns;
- point--triangle incidence; and
- the SRG and prism-free graph laws.

It is not a construction, a local graph module, or evidence that an actual
rank-66 mixed minor exists.

## Association-algebra closure is not available

Nothing currently proves that the mixed tensor depends only on the graph
relations among `x,y,v,z`.  It therefore cannot be placed in the
three-dimensional SRG Bose--Mesner algebra, and no eigenvalue calculation in
that algebra applies.  A genuine coherent-configuration or Terwilliger
closure would have to be derived from the actual labelled graph first.

## Linear sandwich tensors cannot refine a true `A_Delta` relation

For `a=B^Tc in A_Delta`, one has the true operator relation

```text
sum_x c_xP_x=0.
```

Consequently every linear sandwich and mixed pairing vanishes
functorially:

```text
sum_x c_xP_yP_xP_y=0,
sum_x c_xM[(y,x),(v,z)]=0.
```

Mixed traces can distinguish radical false kernels from true kernels, but
they cannot distinguish one true projector relation from another.  An
`A_Delta` classification must use nonlinear or graph-typed data such as the
point coloring `c`, the eight support triangles, their star placements, or
individual coordinate values.

No actual endpoint data establish the rank-66 mixed minor or the rank-25
four-center restriction certificate.  No rank-11 exclusion, `n3`
