# Wave 116 protocol: C4 coordinate theta/Jacobi assessment

## Frozen scope

Condition on the independently verified Wave 66/71 lattice transfer and
Wave 112 four-cycle incidence target.  For every induced four-cycle, inspect
the four coordinate-evaluation vectors in the rank-44 `-4` eigenspace and
decide whether their theta generating functions form a common Jacobi object.

No automorphism, vertex-transitivity, or orbit equivalence of four-cycles is
assumed.

## Gates

1. Check the restricted projector, spectrum, and determinant exactly.
2. Distinguish vectors in `K*` from vectors in `K`; a rational Gram matrix is
   not by itself an ordinary Jacobi index.
3. State the scaling needed for a conventional integral lattice-index
   Jacobi theta series.
4. Track the `K`/`L` Poisson-Fricke partner and its changed index.
5. Track the factor of two between a chosen alternating Fourier pattern and
   oriented vector incidence.
6. Call a finite-dimensional optimization a strategy until an exact basis,
   finite truncation theorem, and dual certificate are produced.
7. Keep rank 28, Conway-99, and literature novelty `UNKNOWN`.
