# Wave 116 failure wall and retained strategy boundary

## Using `E/2` as an ordinary Jacobi index

The projected coordinate vector `u_i/sqrt(7)` is not proved to lie in
`K*`.  Only its triple lies in `K*`, and only twenty-one times the original
coordinate vector lies in `K`.  The small rational Gram `E` cannot be used
as a conventional scalar lattice-Jacobi index without a vector-valued
characteristic completion.

## Closing the 2,401 characteristic sector

The four classes generated by one cycle do form a nondegenerate

orthogonal discriminant complement.  Summing cycles does not identify these
complements or their embeddings; no graph automorphism is available to do

## Treating Fricke as a same-index positivity involution

Poisson summation sends the `K` marking Gram `G_K` to the `L` marking Gram

## Factor-of-two status inflation

The coefficient at the single chosen pattern `(1,-1,1,-1)` counts one
representative of each antipodal incidence.  The oriented incidence is
twice that coefficient.  Ignoring this would overstate the Jacobi
coefficient target by a factor of two.

## Positivity without a finite certificate

Finite-dimensionality alone is not an upper bound.  No exact Jacobi basis,
Sturm truncation, bounded LP, or dual certificate has been constructed.
The only present numerical statement is the imported lower target
`c(7)+c(8)+c(9)>=52812`.

The harmonic interpolation route reduces the index burden but its weighted
theta coefficients are signed.  It needs an independently checked moment or
PSD certificate before it can bound the incidence.
