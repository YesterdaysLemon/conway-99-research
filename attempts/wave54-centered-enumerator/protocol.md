# Wave 54 centered-code enumerator protocol

1. Freeze only the independently integrated Wave 39 conditional code
   consequences: a projective self-orthogonal ternary `[231,11]` code,
   `A_198>=462`, and the four recorded lower bounds on dual weights
   `7,12,13,14`.
2. Require `A_0=1`, total size `3^11`, nonnegative integer coefficients,
   weights divisible by three, and even multiplicity for every nonzero
   weight.
3. Compute all 232 ordinary ternary Krawtchouk rows over exact integers.
4. Require every MacWilliams coefficient to be an integer and nonnegative.
5. Require projectivity `B_1=B_2=0` and the formal self-orthogonality
   inequalities `B_i>=A_i`.
6. Retain a complete nonzero primal and dual coefficient list.
7. Do not call a formal enumerator a code, point set, endpoint matrix, or
   graph.
8. Do not infer that complete-weight, higher-point, or distinguished-star
   constraints are feasible.
9. Discovery cannot promote its own output to `VERIFIED`.
