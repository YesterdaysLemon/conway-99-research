# Protocol

1. Freeze the Wave 21, 22, 43, 141, and 144 inputs by SHA-256.
2. Re-derive the complete per-pattern seventh-vertex admissibility test.
3. Re-enumerate all 62 exact local supports without assuming a graph
   automorphism.
4. Canonicalize rooted patterns only under the automorphism group of the
   induced six-class.
5. Couple those orbit totals to all seven distinguished deletions of every
   one of the 208 locally admissible seven-classes.
6. Impose the complete endpoint deck, Hamiltonian, six-class marginal, and
   Wave 141 moment rows.
7. Use numerical optimization only for discovery.
8. Promote feasibility only after a sparse rational vector replays every
   integer equation exactly.
9. Require a different agent to reconstruct the model and verify the
   witness before publication.
10. Preserve the boundary: aggregate feasibility is not graph realization.
