after coordinate normalization. They prove exact component four-cycle sets
for those sizes. They do not enumerate the `m=8` or `m=12` component graphs,

failure-of-obstruction witnesses. Neither pairing them by the same `q` nor
matching ranks proves that a compatible `B` and `A_Y` exist.

coordinate relabeling of the three fibres. It is a completeness-preserving
normalization of labelled data, not a claim that the relabeling extends to an
automorphism of a graph.
