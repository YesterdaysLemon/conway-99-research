# Boundary and failed promotion routes

## Count-only endpoint exclusion

The complete 208-class order-seven deletion system is exactly feasible at
`n3=4158`. Therefore no linear or integral contradiction using only these
62 deletion equations, nonnegativity, integrality, and the 19 published
Hamiltonian formulas can exclude the endpoint.

This does not prove that every possible order-seven identity is present.
Rooted embeddings, overlap consistency, semidefinite moment constraints, and
unpublished independent identities are outside the model.

## Solver status

The HiGHS status is used only to discover the retained integer vector. The
published mathematical evidence is the exact replay of all equations and
counts. A future negative solver status would remain non-evidence without an
independently checked exact certificate.
