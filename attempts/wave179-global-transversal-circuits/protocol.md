# Protocol

1. Freeze Wave 174 dual distance, Wave 177 pairwise short relations, and
   the independently verified Wave 178 edge refinement.
2. For nonedges, prove existence of a cross circuit inside a cross relation;
   do not select an arbitrary minimal circuit that could be a full star.
3. Define realization using exact two-transversality and require both
   endpoints to occur.
4. Split the realizing-pair family into pairwise-intersecting and disjoint
   cases.
5. Use only triangle uniqueness (`lambda=1`) and three-uniformity in the
   multiplicity proof.
6. Use the verified side balance of each selected edge circuit before
   separating edge circuits from circuits assigned to nonedges.
7. Count projective circuit supports before multiplying by the two nonzero
   ternary scalars.
8. Perform no graph, code, SAT, configuration, or isomorphism search.
9. Require independent verification before promotion.
