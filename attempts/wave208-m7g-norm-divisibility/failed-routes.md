# Failed routes and retained boundaries

* **Literal ternary coefficients as integers.**  Using `2` instead of the
  ordinary lift `-1` destroys the balanced sum needed to remove the `J` term.
  The checker freezes the lift explicitly as four `+1` and four `-1` entries.
* **Guessing intersections from residue one.**  An exact selected-pair entry
  can be one for a disjoint pair or four for an intersecting pair.  The proof
  keeps an unknown intersection sum `s`; its coefficient 18 makes it vanish
  modulo nine.
* **Projectivizing the polar net.**  Replacing a form by twice itself changes
  residue representatives and can change the norm obstruction.  All 27
  affine forms are checked separately.
* **Using norm divisibility as sufficiency.**  Four forms pass the congruence.
  Passing only means that this obstruction does not eliminate them; it does
  not construct the intersection pattern, the outside vertices, or an SRG.
* **Promoting restricted non-hits.**  The 23 exclusions are conditional on
  the prism-free rank-11 endpoint and a hypothetical M7g weight-eight word.
  They do not prove global nonexistence and do not produce a counterexample.
