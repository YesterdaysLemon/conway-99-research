# Boundary and failed continuations

## The new lower bound has no matching upper bound

The conditional inequality `B_4>=4182` is much sharper than a bare
existence statement, but current complete-enumerator constraints do not
provide an incompatible universal upper bound on `B_4`.

## Three block-intersection values remain

Distinct root supports meet in zero, one, or two points.  Strongly regular
design theorems generally need two controlled block intersection values or
a proved relation between intersection size and root orthogonality.  The
present theorem does not supply that last closure.

## Scalar multiplicities are not a construction

The triple `(n_5,n_6,n_7)=(411,4,0)` shows only that the lower bound twelve
is arithmetically sharp.  It does not realize the local `A6` systems,
support intersections, root frame, or graph.
