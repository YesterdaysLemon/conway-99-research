   setting.

   distinct supports.

   orthogonality/intersection equivalence.
4. For each internal support edge, exclude the shared star block and prove
   the four columns are distinct before invoking dual distance four.
5. Use Wave 178--179 isolation to separate graph-edge circuits across edges
   and from canonical nonedge circuits.
6. Count projective circuits before multiplying by the two ternary scalars.
7. Treat the arithmetic witness `(411,4,0)` only as sharpness of the scalar
   bound, not as a geometric construction.
8. Perform no graph, code, SAT, configuration, or isomorphism search.
