# Fourth-order star-pair derivation

## 1. Exact adjacent-pair compression

Work over `F_3`.  For adjacent graph vertices `x,y`, remove their common
triangle and use the six outer star columns as bases of `E_x,E_y`.  Both
diagonal Gram blocks are

```text
G=J_6-I_6,
```

and if `N` is the `6 by 6` biadjacency matrix of the outer cross-incidence
graph, the cross block is

```text
C=J_6+N.
```

Every row and column of `N` has sum two.  Direct multiplication gives

```text
G^-1=2(I+J),
G^-1 C=2N.
```

For the orthogonal projectors onto the two nondegenerate star spaces,
the compression of `P_x P_y P_x` to `E_x` is therefore

```text
A_xy
 =G^-1 C G^-1 C^T
 =N N^T.                                         (1)
```

This is the promised small exact module.  It depends only on the partition
of six giving the cycle half-lengths.

Because `P_x^2=P_x`, cyclicity of trace gives the full-space identity

```text
tr(A_xy^2)
 =tr((P_x P_y P_x)^2)
 =tr(P_x P_y P_x P_y).                           (2)
```

Thus there is no ambiguity between the compressed and full fourth traces.

## 2. Four exact modules

For a component of half-length `m`, choose

```text
N_m=I+S_m,
A_m=N_m N_m^T=2I+S_m+S_m^-1.
```

Block summing over the parts gives:

| type | rank of `A_xy` | characteristic polynomial | minimal polynomial |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `6` | 4 | `x^3(x-1)^3` | `x^2(x-1)^2` |
| `4+2` | 4 | `x^2(x-1)^2(x+1)^2` | `x(x-1)(x+1)` |
| `3+3` | 6 | `(x-1)^6` | `(x-1)^2` |
| `2+2+2` | 3 | `x^3(x-1)^3` | `x(x-1)` |

The checker obtains these polynomials directly from the displayed matrices,
using exact modular determinant expansion and row-reduced power
dependencies.

Every type has

```text
tr(A_xy)=tr(P_x P_y)=0.
```

For `m>=3`, the diagonal contributions in `A_m^2` sum to zero in `F_3`.
An `m=2` component contributes one.  Hence

```text
tr(A_xy^2)=number of 2-parts mod 3.
```

Among the four allowed partitions this is

```text
type                         6  4+2  3+3  2+2+2
tr(P_x P_y P_x P_y)         0   1    0     0.    (3)
```

The last zero is essential: three `2`-parts vanish in characteristic three.
Equation (3) detects the `4+2` type but is not an integer count of
two-components.

## 3. Exterior- and symmetric-square forms

For `g_xy=tr(P_xP_y)` and
`h_xy=tr(P_xP_yP_xP_y)`, functoriality and Newton's identity give

```text
tr((wedge^2 P_x)(wedge^2 P_y))=(g_xy^2-h_xy)/2,
tr((Sym^2 P_x)(Sym^2 P_y))  =(g_xy^2+h_xy)/2.
```

On graph edges `g_xy=0`.  Since `1/2=2` in `F_3`,

```text
exterior trace=h_xy,
symmetric trace=2h_xy.
```

Thus the exterior-square trace is exactly the same `4+2` detector.

## 4. Exact positive controls against a pairwise leap

The checker gives three explicit plus-type star projectors `P,Q1,Q2` in
one nondegenerate 11-space.  Each is generated by seven singular columns
with Gram `J_7-I_7`, has rank six and trace zero, and satisfies
`P=-sum(z tensor z)`.  The pairs obey

```text
                         tr(PQ)  dim(im P intersect im Q)  tr(PQPQ)
P,Q1                        2                 2                1
P,Q2                        2                 2                2
```

So even pair trace plus intersection dimension does not fix fourth trace.

For a stronger endpoint-shaped control, take 33 seven-column groups:
9 of type `P`, 12 of type `Q1`, and 12 of type `Q2`.  Give each group to
three projector labels.  Swapping the `Q1` and `Q2` assignments produces a
second control.  Both have:

- 99 projector labels and 231 column labels;
- ambient and centered-Gram rank 11;
- singular columns and square-zero centered Gram;
- seven simplex columns per star and column incidence degree three;
- zero sum of all projectors; and
- exactly the same labelled `99 by 99` pairwise trace Gram.

Their labelled fourth-trace matrices differ in 3,888 ordered entries.
This is an exact countermodel to determining the fourth tensor from the
listed algebraic data.

The controls repeat projective directions and have a non-linear,
non-graph incidence matrix.  They are not endpoint configurations.  The
full failed-premise list is frozen in `exact-results.json`.

## 5. Boundary

Equation (3) is a genuine graph-specific adjacent-pair invariant.  It does
not determine how many graph edges have type `4+2`, constrain the nonedge
fourth traces, or conflict with a dimension/rank bound.

The sharply exposed missing invariant is:

```text
the graph-specific distribution and global compatibility of
h_xy=tr(P_xP_yP_xP_y), including every nonedge pair.
```

No endpoint exclusion, strict improvement of `n3<=4158`, graph
construction, or Conway-99 resolution follows.
