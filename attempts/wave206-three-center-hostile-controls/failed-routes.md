The real `p`-fusion-frame and cubature theorems require positive weights and
proved polynomial exactness on a compact Grassmannian. The ternary identity
`sum_x P_x=0` supplies neither hypothesis. Importing the real theorem would
assume the missing moment condition.

Disposition: `REFUTED` as an inference from the frozen premises.

## Treating strong regularity as triple regularity

Triple regularity controls common-relation counts for a labeled triple.
Suda's cited consequences use tight spherical-design or separate dual
conditions. They do not state that every strongly regular graph is triply
regular, and they do not identify the ternary projector trace with a triple
intersection number.

Disposition: `REFUTED` as an unconditional literature transfer.

not provide the missing adjacency actions, and the disconnected hostile
