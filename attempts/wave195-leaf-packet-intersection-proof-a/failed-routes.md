# Wave195 failed and null routes

These routes are retained to prevent status inflation or repetition.

## Mixed-orientation canonical leaf-support route: gap

For a type-three-oriented packet, proper-star independence can force the
assigned exact-one raw to fill the canonical all-equal `3+6` weight-nine
leaf word.

The analogous type-one argument did **not** close. After subtracting an
exact-three raw `D` from a majority translate `W`, equality may force a
residual containing the companion `D*`. A second elimination need not
produce a fourth circuit: its remainder can be the already selected
exact-one owner `C_e`, and subtraction can reintroduce the coordinate
initially omitted from `C_e`. The existing coefficient checker did not
classify arbitrary `C_e/W` containment.

Therefore the claims that every mixed packet has canonical weight-nine
leaf owners and that every nonedge star-space intersection is
one-dimensional were withdrawn. They are not used in the successful
Hilton--Milner proof.

## Nonedge relation-code averaging: scoped only

If one independently knows that the three equality circuits through every
nonedge span a full-support three-dimensional local code, the
outside-subcode average is `28/3` and forces one-dimensional intersections
of nonadjacent star spaces. That full-support premise was established only
for the canonical all-type-three orientation, not for the mixed equality
face. The repeated-leaf-triangle clique contradiction is therefore only a
scoped corroboration, not the Wave195 theorem.

## Pairwise leaf-word differences

For one canonical flag `(x,{y,u,v})`,

```text
w_y-w_u=2*(s_y-s_u).
```

The result is the 12-coordinate outer-star relation for graph edge `yu`.
Circuit extraction first returns the already known edge-circuit sector.
This identity alone does not force a new nonedge circuit.

The candidate five-circuit packet inventory plus the 693 edge circuits
reaches the verified Wave188 lower bound of 9,009 projective short words.
That theorem is a lower bound, not an upper bound, so numerical saturation
is not a contradiction.

A Gram-kernel vector need not be a true column relation when the restricted

## Hoffman/design boundary

The flag-incidence equation and its Hoffman bound admit a uniform rational
control. They do not alone exclude an integral flag partition. The
successful proof instead maps each center to an extremal set-system
problem and invokes the Hilton--Milner stability threshold.
