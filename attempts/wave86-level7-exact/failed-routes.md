formal modular pair attains that total through the full Sturm coordinate
range.  Its checked continuation is integral, even, and nonnegative through

`L` and `K` are exchanged by Fricke.  Neither theta series is individually
a Fricke eigenform unless an additional isometry is supplied.  No such
isometry is imported.

## Vector-valued and harmonic theta series

The discriminant-form representation depends on more than the elementary
group `(Z/7)^16`; its exact quadratic-form isometry type and coset
enumerators are not present in Wave 66/71.  Harmonic theta series of the
marked 99-frame likewise require marked polynomial moments not determined
by the scalar theta coefficients.  These routes need additional exact
input and are not used to inflate (3).
