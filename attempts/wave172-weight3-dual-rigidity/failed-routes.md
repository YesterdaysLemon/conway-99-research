# Boundary and failed shortcuts

## Ordinary `B_3` is not fixed

Wave 54 exhibits one formally feasible ordinary enumerator with `B_3=120`.
It does not prove that every admissible ordinary enumerator has this value.
Consequently, proving that mixed-sign weight-three words are absent is not
by itself an endpoint contradiction.

## Pairwise orthogonality is not dependence

The support of a weight-three dual word is a triangle in the relation
`D_ij=0`, but three pairwise orthogonal isotropic projective points can span a
three-space.  Counting triangles in that relation would overcount actual
linear dependencies unless collinearity is also certified.

## No existence claim for a `3K3` dependency

The theorem states what a weight-three dependency would look like.  It does
not assert that every induced `3K3`, or any induced `3K3`, supplies one.

## Live continuation

A complete or partition weight-enumerator argument can now impose zero
coefficients for dual compositions `(2,1)` and `(1,2)`, together with the
231 distinguished primal compositions `(162,36)` and `(36,162)` and the 99
point-star dual words.  Any infeasibility result must be exact and
independently verified.
