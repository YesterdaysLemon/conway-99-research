generalized-unit fixed point. Exactly 830 variables remain forced, including
174 primary residual-edge variables, and zero derivations come from the new
delta. The delta therefore strengthens the branch without supplying a
zero-search contradiction.

Together with the prior six families it still omits prisms whose two triangles
are both unfixed. A satisfiable assignment of this partial formula is not a
prism-free endpoint graph.
