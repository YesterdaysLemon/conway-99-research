# Wave 166 protocol

## Goal

Transform the missing endpoint inequality into a rooted local extension
problem that can plausibly yield a lower bound on the induced Wagner count.

## Lanes

1. Fixed `C4`: derive every exact shell degree equation, classify cube and
   Wagner patterns, and construct a null shell if the equations are
   insufficient.
2. Fixed `C5`: use `sum e_W=8W8` to state the exact aggregate lower target.
3. Fixed nonedge: count marked `C5`s exactly and isolate a local failure
   bound sufficient for endpoint exclusion.

## Separation

- Exact identities and null models may be `DERIVED`.
- The four-failure lemma stays `UNKNOWN` until independently proved.
- An abstract shell is not a graph completion.
- A sufficient lower-bound target is not evidence that the target holds.
- No agent may promote its own discovery.

reserve. Any future checker must wait for at least 18% free RAM and stop
