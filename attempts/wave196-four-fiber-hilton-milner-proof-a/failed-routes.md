# Wave196 failed and superseded routes

## Parity at the old local cap

At the Wave195 bound `j_x<=39`, equality suggested centers of size 13 or
15 and a possible parity obstruction. The four-element type fibers make
that analysis obsolete: nontrivial size-13 families force repeats, and a
common-star family has at most twelve flags. No parity claim is used.

## The affine envelope `2*j_x<=63+c_x`

The first stability refinement only excluded `j_x=39` in a nontrivial
family and produced an affine deficit row. Inspecting all three
degree-five pairs in both Hilton--Milner equality templates improves the
local bound directly to `j_x<=36`, so the affine row is retained only as a
superseded intermediate observation.

## Global distinguished-triangle double count

An attempted global count assigned a common star triangle to every
saturated center. The local `mu=2`/`lambda=1` reconstruction already shows
that such a common-star family has at most twelve flags, so no global
witness count is needed.

## Arithmetic sharpness

The new linear certificate has an integer arithmetic null row at
`Q0=7029`. It is not a construction and gives no evidence that the local
four-fiber equality geometry can be glued globally.
