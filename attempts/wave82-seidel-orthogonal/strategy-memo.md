not calibrated forecasts.

## Selected route

The Seidel route was selected because it is disjoint from the active Wave 80
and Wave 81 work, adds an exact unrestricted representation, and supports a
small standard-library verifier.

## Rejected rediscovery

The norm-14 support was independently shifted into a colored
`2-(15,3,2)` design and a 9-regular graph on 70 vertices.  Repository
comparison showed this is exactly the already verified Wave 33/34 rooted
extension, including the `-1+/-sqrt(2)` and residual `3,-4` spectrum.  It is
therefore recorded as an exhausted route, not a Wave 82 result.
