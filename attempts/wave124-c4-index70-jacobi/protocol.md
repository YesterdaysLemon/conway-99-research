# Wave 124 protocol

## Frozen scope

Condition on the sealed verifier packages for Waves 71, 96, 112, and 116.
For each induced C4, use only its alternating cyclic sign vector.  Do not
assume any graph automorphism, vertex transitivity, or equivalence of
different cycles.

## Required checks

1. Prove `d_C` lies in `M subset L`, and compute its norm, primitivity, and
   the divisibility of `sqrt(7)d_C` in `K`.
2. Derive the one-variable K/L Jacobi indices and Poisson-Fricke
   normalization without treating Fricke as a fixed-index involution.
3. Check whether `r=28` has false positives on every shell used, including
   norm 20.
4. Track oriented versus antipodal counts.
5. Derive the aggregate C4 frame and Jacobi second moment independently of
   graph symmetry.
6. Construct only basis spaces justified by a cited structure theorem.
   Never promote a full-level subspace to the whole level-seven space.
7. Treat a modular direction as a null control, not a graph construction,
   unless positivity, integrality, and all graph support zeros hold.
8. Accept an upper bound only through an exact finite model and dual
   certificate.

## Status wall

Discovery cannot verify itself.  No finite search nonhit is evidence of
nonexistence.  Rank exclusions, Conway-99, and novelty remain `UNKNOWN`
unless separately certified.
