# Limitations and failed routes

## The spectral/Tanner count stops at the classical floor

Ignoring signs, a check node cannot meet the support exactly once.  The
standard first/second moment argument then gives only `w>=8`.  It does not
use the forced cancellation between the two nonzero ternary symbols.

## Signed moments improve the floor but do not reach 24

The signed Farkas argument proves `d>=12`.  Exact aggregate integer controls
exist at weights 12, 13, and 14 for the first/second moment system.  The
submitted weight-fourteen control also satisfies aggregate `7K_2`
matching-sum equations.  It is deliberately nongraphical, so it neither
proves nor refutes the existence of a codeword.  It does prove that those
aggregate equations alone cannot close the route.

## No minimum-distance theorem was imported

No parameter-only theorem establishing `d(ker_F3 A)>=24` was identified in
the frozen sources.  Treating the visible adjacent closed-neighborhood
differences of weight 24 as minimum words would be circular.

## The image-zero caveat is resolved only at weight eight

For the audited `4+4` word, `a.a=c^T b=2` proves `b!=0`.  This argument uses
the exact composition and does not say that the point image of every word in
`im(B^T)` is nonzero.

## No global conclusion

The bridge is conditional on the Wave 206 rank-eleven endpoint word.  Even a
future proof excluding that endpoint would not by itself settle all possible
values of `n3`, and the present package does not exclude it.
