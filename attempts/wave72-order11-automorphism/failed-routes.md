# Failed routes and scope boundaries

## Treating the fixed graph as regular

The induced fixed graph need not be regular a priori: its vertex degrees can
be either 3 or 14.  The proof retains both types and uses the local two-step
identity before eliminating mixtures.

## Assuming fixed pairs keep their common neighbors

This is not automatic for arbitrary automorphism order or arbitrary common-
neighbor counts.  Here it is valid only because the invariant common-neighbor
sets have sizes 1 or 2, both strictly less than the prime orbit size 11.

## Assuming vertex transitivity

No transitivity assumption is used in the fixed-point theorem.  Transitivity
appears only in the final conditional corollary, where orbit-stabilizer and
Cauchy's theorem would force an order-11 automorphism.

## Reusing Wave 69 as if already verified

Wave 69 is a separate discovery package.  This package does not certify its
search completeness or implementation.  The no-order-11 and
non-vertex-transitive conclusions remain conditional pending independent
verification of both lanes.

## Global nonexistence

Even the conditional symmetry exclusions leave asymmetric targets in scope.
They are not evidence of unrestricted nonexistence.
