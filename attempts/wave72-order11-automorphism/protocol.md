# Frozen protocol

## Target

Assume that `G` is a finite simple undirected strongly regular graph with
parameters

```text
(v,k,lambda,mu) = (99,14,1,2).
```

Let `g` be an automorphism whose permutation order is exactly 11.

## Primary claim

Prove that `g` fixes no vertex.

## Conditional corollary lane

Only if the independent Wave 69 claim excluding every semiregular order-11
action is verified may the two packages be combined to exclude order-11
automorphisms.  Only then may orbit-stabilizer and Cauchy's theorem be used to
exclude vertex transitivity.

## Status rules

- The primary theorem is labeled `DERIVED` until a different agent reproduces
  and attacks the proof.
- Wave 69 is not reverified by this package.
- No conclusion is drawn about asymmetric graphs or automorphisms of other
  orders.
- The unrestricted Conway-99 existence question remains `UNKNOWN`.
