# Hostile routes that did not refute the theorem

## Companion double counting

The two exact-three circuits on one canonical flag realize the same three
labels. Selecting both violates minimal-cover privacy, so one flag
contributes at most one selected circuit.

## Opposite orientation on a private label

A second selected occurrence in the opposite orientation would make the
unordered label nonprivate. Thus a private label contributes
`s_e=t_e=1`.

## Old/raw collision with the selected union

The old raw assignments are keyed by private type-one/type-two source
labels outside the selected type-three union. The type-two pair uses
opposite orientations rather than duplicating one oriented label.
