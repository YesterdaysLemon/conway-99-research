# Failed or incomplete routes

1. **Ordinary vertex-nullity coefficient nonnegativity.** The only
   decreasing order-six coefficient gives `n3<=156737735`, far weaker than
   `4158`.
2. **All local diagonal toggles.** Splitting by the number of `psi` choices
   and nullity improves the interlace-only number to `7609140`, still far
   weaker than `4158`.
3. **Wave141-only elimination.** The kernel-size moment requires the
   `h=0` slice of
   `h=|supp(x) intersect supp(Ax)|`. Wave141 stores only the sum over `h`.
   No exact inequality eliminating the new slice back to the bivariate
   table was derived.
4. **Full interlace/isotropic enumeration.** This was not attempted: without
   a graph it would require a new global formal enumerator, and direct
   `2^99` or `3^99` enumeration is prohibited and infeasible.

These are null results, not evidence that a stronger intersection-refined
approach cannot work.
