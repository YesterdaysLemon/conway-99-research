# Wave 191 proof-A protocol

1. Freeze the verified Wave180, Wave181, Wave189, and Wave190 packages by
   SHA-256 before deriving anything.
2. Work only under the conditional prism-free rank-11 endpoint assumptions.
3. Treat exact-three circuits through the verified companion classification;
   do not infer a circuit merely from a singular Gram support.
4. Analyze the two possible centers of an exact-three raw circuit inside a
   fixed `3+6` leaf relation.
5. Use proper-star independence to exclude the owner center.
6. In the leaf-center case, derive the unique `uv` source block and prove
   `T in A_y`; do not retain a spurious `T in B_y` branch.
7. Compare the resulting all-equal four-word with the verified canonical
   checkerboard kernel before promoting it to a residual circuit.
8. Close genuinely new exact-three residuals under companionship and count
   a pair at three labels for two circuits.
9. Couple type-two and type-three consumption of the same exact-one slots.
10. Keep the scalar arithmetic row distinct from an object construction.
11. Use the exact checker only for fixed coefficient vectors and identities;
   run no graph, cover, code, SAT, LP, or isomorphism search.
12. Leave the result `DERIVED` until an independent verifier reproduces it
    without relying on discovery internals.
