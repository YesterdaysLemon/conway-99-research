# Compact hostile derivation

Forward `x->y` flags inject from the five `y`-side candidate leaf blocks
to five third-block slots in `C_x(y)`. Reverse flags use their leaf block
as a domain slot in the same `C_x(y)`.

If a reverse flag occupies a forward image slot `S`, the leaf-type rule
gives `j(S,T)=2` and forces reverse third block `T`. The two
leaf-normalized relations sum to the all-equal word on the four canonical
common-neighbor blocks. Their Gram has checkerboard kernel and rejects
all-equal. Hence matched occupancy is impossible and combined capacity is
five.

Selected flags are a subset of the full pool, so unordered selected
multiplicity is at most five. Private labels have multiplicity one,
yielding `3n3+4p3<=5|U|`. A both-oriented label contributes at least five
to `epsilon`.

No current row forces a both-oriented label, so no 7,060 claim follows.
