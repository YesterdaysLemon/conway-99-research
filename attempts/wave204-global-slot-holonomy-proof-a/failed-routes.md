# Failed routes and retained obstructions

## 1. Center-cycle holonomy is not defined

Projecting a flag edge from triangle blocks to its ordered pair of centers
forgets the source and target block identities.  Consecutive center labels do
not make consecutive block arrows.  Any proof that sums gains around a center
triangle, quadrilateral, or pentagon without proving the intermediate block
equalities has inserted an unproved gluing axiom.

## 2. A partial injection is not a permutation

Wave 203 explicitly forbids inflating its partial third-block map to a total
bijection.  The exact identity/transposition extension pair shows that unused
slots can change full `S_5` monodromy without changing one supplied partial
value.

## 3. Zero block holonomy is tautological

On a genuine block cycle, the gain is `z_T-z_S`, so it telescopes.  This
cannot by itself contradict rank 11 or the endpoint.  A new obstruction would
need gains not already given by one global potential, or additional
restrictions on which block cycles must exist.

## 4. The canonical C4 Gram is not a universal 2-cell

The checkerboard word spans the canonical Gram kernel, but the frozen branch
does not say that every canonical quadrilateral is dependent.  Treating every
such square as a relation would import the old Wave 181 equality hypothesis
outside its scope.

## 5. The relaxed controls are not endpoint objects

The exact rank-11 controls deliberately stop at local stars and flag
relations.  They omit 99-star/231-column incidence, the global frame sum, SRG
axioms, and cover totals.  Their nonzero projected defects refute a local
logical implication; they do not construct a graph or code.
