cannot extend to the Wave132 split system.

## Calling the split system genus two

The package does not impose the full partial-Hadamard transform.  It keeps
only pair compositions of the distinguished rows and per-shell row sums,
first moments, parity counts, and intersection ranges.  Calling this a
full genus-two enumerator would overstate the result.

## Live continuation

A complete next layer must couple the 7,803 `GL(2,2)` composition orbits
through exact partial-Hadamard transforms and enforce nonnegative
coefficients in `C x C`, `C x D`, and `D x D`.  A sparse orbit transform
or a dual Farkas search is preferable to materializing the dense
42,925-state matrix.
