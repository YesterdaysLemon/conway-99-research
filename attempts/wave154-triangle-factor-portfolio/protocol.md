# Protocol

- Wave151 manifest and exact result are frozen inputs.
- Symmetry claims are checked by explicit enumeration of all 384 maps.
- A Q1 construction is accepted only by exact binary Gram replay.
- A full C construction requires direct replay of all three diagonal and
  cross blocks.
- Fixed-Q1 UNSAT claims remain branch-local.
- Negative status requires an exported proof and independent proof check.
- Timeouts, interruptions, no-incumbent results, and missing proofs remain
  UNKNOWN.
- D is not entered before a complete C exists.
