# Distinct balanced short circuits indexed by edges

## 1. Frozen setting

Assume the prism-free endpoint `P=0` and the smallest surviving centered
rank `k=11`.  Let `W` be the ternary code generated by the 231 centered
triangle-block columns `z_T`.

The inputs are:

1. Wave 174: `d(W^perp)>=4`.
2. Wave 176: each seven-column point-star has exactly its full-support
   one-dimensional circuit; hence every proper subset of a star is
   independent.
3. Wave 177: for every graph edge `xy`, after deleting its unique common
   triangle `T_xy`, the 12 outer columns support a true cross-star relation
   of weight `4..8`.

There are `99*14/2=693` graph edges.

## 2. Pass from a relation to a circuit

Fix an edge `xy` and one Wave 177 relation.  Choose an inclusion-minimal
dependent subset `C_xy` of its support.  It is a matroid circuit and still
lies among the 12 outer columns.

It cannot lie in just one six-column outer star, because that is a proper
subset of a seven-column star and is independent.  Thus `C_xy` meets both
outer stars.  Wave 174 and containment in the original relation give

```text
4 <= |C_xy| <= 8.                                  (1)
```

## 3. One outer circuit support serves at most one edge

Let a nonzero relation support `C` satisfy (1), meet both sides, and be
contained in the two outer stars of an edge.  Suppose it also serves a
second edge.

### Shared endpoint

Write the two edges as `xy` and `xz`.  Because `C` meets both sides for
`xy`, choose a support triangle `T` containing `y` and not `x`.  Relative
to `xz`, the same triangle does not contain `x`, so it must contain `z`.
Thus `T={y,z,t}` for some `t`.

The edge `yz` then has both `x` and `t` as common neighbors.  They are
distinct because `x` is not in `T`, contradicting `lambda=1`.

### Disjoint endpoints

Write the two edges as `x_0 x_1` and `y_0 y_1`.  Every support triangle
contains exactly one `x_i` and exactly one `y_j`.  There are only four
endpoint-pair patterns `(x_i,y_j)`.  A fixed pair occurs in at most one
triangle, because an adjacent pair has exactly one common neighbor.
Therefore `|C|<=4`.

By (1), equality holds and all four patterns occur.  Both `y_0` and `y_1`
are then common neighbors of the edge `x_0 x_1`, again contradicting
`lambda=1`.

Hence every such support determines at most one edge.  Choosing one
minimal circuit for each edge is therefore injective on projective circuit
classes.

## 4. The short circuits are balanced and even

For an adjacent pair, order the 13 columns as

```text
common block | six x-outer blocks | six y-outer blocks.
```

The outer cross-incidence graph is 2-regular bipartite on `6+6` vertices,
so its cycle half-length partition is exactly one of

```text
6, 4+2, 3+3, 2+2+2.
```

Every true column relation lies in the kernel of the corresponding Gram
matrix.  Set the common coordinate to zero, remove the span of the two
full-star circuits, and retain weight at most eight.  Exact reduction of
the four `13 x 13` matrices gives:

```text
cycle type   eligible Gram-kernel words
6            6  of profile (8;4,4;4,4)
4+2          2  of profile (4;2,2;2,2)
             2  of profile (8;4,4;4,4)
3+3         12  of profile (4;2,2;2,2)
             4  of profile (6;3,3;3,3)
            36  of profile (8;4,4;4,4)
2+2+2        6  of profile (4;2,2;2,2)
            12  of profile (8;4,4;4,4)
```

Here `(w;a,b;n_1,n_2)` records total weight, support sizes in the two
outer stars, and the counts of coefficients `1` and `2=-1`.
Consequently every true circuit obtained in Section 2 has size `4`, `6`,
or `8`, has half its support in each star, and is coefficient-balanced.
The finite check is only a transparent classification of four already
derived local kernels; it is not a construction search.

## 5. Enumerator consequence

There are at least 693 distinct projective dual circuits of weights
`4,6,8`.  Each projective class has the two nonzero scalar representatives
over `F_3`.  If `B_i` denotes the number of weight-`i` words of `W^perp`,
then

```text
B_4+B_6+B_8 >= 2*693 = 1386.                       (2)
```

## Boundary

Equation (2) is a necessary condition for the conditional rank-11
endpoint.  It is not a contradiction.  The radius-one dual Hamming bound
has `1+2*231=463 << 3^11=177147`; elementary circuit elimination does not
force a relation below the verified dual distance four.  The local cycle
types are not globally equidistant, and the short dual circuits do not
supply the primal full-support subcode required by critical-exponent
theorems.

No strict improvement to `n3<=4158`, rank-11 exclusion, graph construction,
or Conway-99 resolution follows.
