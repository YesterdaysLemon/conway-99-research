# Failed routes and retained boundaries

## Dividing by two

This route is impossible.  The marked equations `U^Tq=-3alpha` are odd, so
`q` is not even.  The norm-14 complementary-Fano theorem cannot be imported
for `q/2`.

## Scalar residual norm

The residual norm 96 does not contradict integrality.  The exact projector
bound for the full triangle vector is saturated at 168, and all residual
values lie in `{-2,-1,0,1,2}`.  Norm feasibility is necessary but not a graph.

## Polar-signature and selected-row aggregation

Adding exact one- and two-way selected intersection counts, all polar first
and second moments, and all eight selected rows of `Ct=0` still leaves an
integer aggregate control in every one of the 24 proved relabeling orbits.
The controls cover all 249 labelled branches.  This negative result must not
be promoted to a 223-block or 99-vertex construction.

The subsequent 99-point joint signature layer is stronger and does exclude
198 labelled branches.  The statement above applies only to the coarser
triangle-side aggregation.

## Selected-union integer assignments

Every branch has a bounded integer assignment satisfying its eight marked
triangle sums with norm at most 34.  These are hostile controls against a
marked-line or parity-only contradiction.  They omit every outside adjacency
equation and therefore do not witness an integral `-4` eigenvector.

## Short-vector classifications

No repository classification at norms 14, 16, 18, or 20 is extrapolated to
norm 56.  The point-projector bound only gives `|q_x|<=4`; the remaining 800
arithmetic coordinate-count profiles are not eigenlattice vectors.

## Surviving point-signature censuses

Seven relabeling orbits, containing 51 labelled branches, have explicit
integer 99-row signature censuses satisfying every single-coordinate and
pairwise table plus the norm.  These controls are not labelled graph points:
they do not encode adjacency between rows or check `Aq=-4q`.  Feasibility of
the census is therefore not feasibility of the endpoint.
