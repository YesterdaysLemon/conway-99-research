## Primary theorem used

Nils-Peter Skoruppa, *Reduction mod \(\ell\) of Theta Series of Level
\(\ell^n\)*, arXiv:0807.4694v1 (2008).

- Primary source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.4694>
- Frozen query: `site:arxiv.org modular lattices level 7 minimum bound theta
  series dimension 44`
- Exact imported statement: Main Theorem in section 1.  For an even integral
  lattice of level a power of a prime \(\ell\ge5\), there is an integral
  level-one modular form congruent modulo \(\ell\) to its theta series, of
  weight \(e(L)/2\), where \(e(L)\) is the sum of the elementary divisors.

The article also explicitly records
\(\mathbf Z_{(\ell)}[E_4,E_6]\) for integral level-one modular forms and
\(\widetilde E_6=1\) when \(\ell=7\).  Wave 71 independently recomputes the
needed \(E_4,E_6\) coefficients and all row reductions.
