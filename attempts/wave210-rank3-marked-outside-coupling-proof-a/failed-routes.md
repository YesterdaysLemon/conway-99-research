# Failed and nonclosing routes

## Column containment alone

Coupling each marked zero only to a column containing its two incident
support blocks leaves all 232 distinct requirement patterns.  The weakest
pattern still has 422 compatible deficit bijections and the strongest has
2,116.  Containment without the 28 selected-line equations is not an
exclusion method.

## Aggregate column signatures

The global counts `(z_0,z_1,z_2)=(17,61,7)` do not determine which support
pairs occur in a column.  The successful cut requires the exact 7-by-7
two-support multiplicity matrix for each of the 4,480 deficit bijections.

## Treating the local one-factor as unique

The local one-factor condition is forced, but its solution is not.  Exact
neighborhoods admit 68, 78, 90, 594, or 604 perfect matchings.  Any argument
that silently chooses one local matching would add an unjustified
restriction.

## Selected-union non-hit as a global claim

The full coupling eliminates 30 of 33 proved case orbits, but three explicit
orbits survive.  Conversely, those three 19-point controls do not imply a
completion: the 85-by-85 outside adjacency block remains absent.

## Unproved target automorphisms

No target automorphism was used.  A cache was introduced only after all
20,928 labelled cases were generated and partitioned under symmetries checked
directly on the frozen polar matrix and fixed support graph.  Quotienting the
204 marked graphs earlier would not have been certified.
