## 1. The exact 85 support columns

Let `S=P union N` be the fourteen nonzero coordinates of the Wave 209
eigenvector, with `|P|=|N|=7`.  On either sign side the rooted support graph

```text

```

and the only support cross edge is the edge joining the two roots.  The seven
same-sign common-neighbor deficit edges are

```text
14 15 23 26 35 46 56.                            (1)
```

Write `F` for the `14 x 85` support/outside incidence matrix.  The Wave 209
moments force 17 zero columns, 61 columns containing one point of each sign,
and seven columns containing two points of each sign.  A four-support column
pairs one edge of (1) on `P` with one edge of (1) on `N`.  Of all `7! = 5040`
pairings, exactly 4,480 leave every cross-sign common-neighbor residual
nonnegative.  For any retained pairing, that residual `7 x 7` matrix is the
exact multiplicity matrix of the 61 two-support columns.  There is no further
choice in the multiset.

The checker verifies for every one of the 4,480 multisets that

```text
F F^T = 12 I - A_S + 2 J - A_S^2.                (2)
```

It also obtains `rank_Q(F)=13`; the left kernel is generated by the signed
support vector `(1^7,-1^7)`.


```text

```


## 4. The forced local one-factor condition

For every support vertex `s`, the local graph on its fourteen neighbors must
be 1-regular because adjacent vertices have exactly one common neighbor.
Known support/support and support/outside adjacencies already pair some of
those neighbors.  Every remaining outside neighbor of `s` must be paired
with another outside column whose intersection with it inside the support is
exactly `{s}`.  This is a forced perfect-matching condition, although the
perfect matching itself need not be unique.

An independent bitmask matching reconstruction checks all 14 support
neighborhoods in all 4,480 exact `F` configurations.  Every configuration
admits all fourteen matchings.  Across the 62,720 individual neighborhoods,
the numbers of perfect matchings are distributed as

```text

 90:  1,536
594: 18,496
604: 17,344.                                      (12)
```

The mandatory selected-line zero/zero edges from every survivor in (9) also
extend through these local one-factors.  Consequently this exact local test
does not reduce (10).
