# Failed routes and cautions

## Internal short circuits are not incidence words

The twelve weight-four and eight weight-five circuits inside the `M_7g`
support are genuine relations among the centered columns.  This alone does
not put their coefficient words in `im(B^T)`, so orthogonality to
`ker(B)` cannot be applied to them.

## Product one remains ambiguous

For two selected triangle blocks, a centered product zero or two forces the
blocks to be disjoint.  Product one does not determine intersection: it is
also produced by a disjoint pair with exactly one cross edge.  Any argument
that identifies every product-one pair is invalid.

## Local compatibility is not a completion

An induced graph on the vertices occurring in the eight selected triangles
can check the equations whose nonzero vector `b=Ba` is supported there.
It does not supply the other 91 graph vertices, the other 223 triangle
blocks, the full strongly regular common-neighbor equalities, or a rank-11
231-column frame.
