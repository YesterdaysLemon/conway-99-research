# Failed and bounded routes

## Raw 19-triangle coherent closure

The root plus its eighteen `K`-neighbors has six initial directed pair colors.
Exact 2-WL stabilizes without a proper refinement round. Thus the raw forced
relations reveal no new color or intersection-number obstruction.

## Treating one `B/C` completion as forced

This is invalid. The 64 canonical cycle-profile diagnostics already produce
39 different stable intersection fingerprints, with stable color counts from
8 through 361. A chosen completed closure is evidence only about that chosen
local object.

## Treating the cap-incidence closure as a CSP solver

The 163-node incidence template refines from 26 to 47 colors, but 2-WL does
not choose truth values for candidate `B` nodes. The diagonal classes remain
exactly the four node roles of sizes `1,18,36,108`. Explicit integral
2-factor completions satisfy every exact-two cap, so no contradiction is
available at this level.

## Treating local feasibility as graph evidence

Also invalid. The cap system omits compatibility among different root
triangles, the remaining 212 triangle vertices, and the full 99-vertex SRG
adjacency equations. A local completion need not lift to any Conway graph.
