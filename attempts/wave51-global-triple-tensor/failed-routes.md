# Retained limitations and failed routes

## Local signed tables alone

For each pair relation, the transportation margins, `K^2`, `KS=4K`, the
signed square identity, and the local `3K6` rule admit nonnegative integral
tables. Thus no contradiction can come from checking these equations one
pair at a time.

## Globally symmetric triple counts

Coupling the five local polytopes by exact triple-count symmetry also remains
feasible. The explicit tensor in `exact-result.json` is a positive aggregate
control. This closes the current linear triple-count lane as a null result.

## Homogeneous association-scheme interpretation

The displayed average tables fail 100 of 625 associativity equations. Hence
they cannot be used as constant intersection numbers of an association
scheme. This is only a refutation of that stronger interpretation. A general
endpoint graph could have varying local tables with the same averages, so the
failure is not an endpoint obstruction.

## Unattempted stronger systems

No exact quadruple tensor, Terwilliger algebra, coherent-configuration
closure, or realizability model was built. Those would need variables that
retain pair-to-pair variation rather than only averages.

No absence-of-object claim is made. The prism-free endpoint and Conway-99
remain `UNKNOWN`.
