# Protocol

- Role: statement/literature plus hostile construction.
- Discovery work may label the scoped lemma `REFUTED_PENDING_INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION`;
  it may not promote itself to `VERIFIED`.
- All arithmetic is exact integer arithmetic.
- The generator is deterministic and emits a complete JSON certificate.
- The verifier reconstructs every graph degree, local family, fiber
  partition, selected count, multiplicity, slot map, and orientation.
- Scope flags are fail-closed: the artifact explicitly denies being an SRG,
  ternary column realization, rank-11 code, circuit cover, or endpoint object.
- No automorphism of a hypothetical Conway graph is assumed. The cyclic
  symmetry belongs only to the explicit relaxed countermodel.
- Primary-source nonhits are not novelty or openness evidence.
