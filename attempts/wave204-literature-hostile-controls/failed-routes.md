# Failed routes and retained boundaries

## A sheaf cannot be inferred from slot counts

The frozen data gives a partial injection from existing flags to five
third-block slots. It does not give a restriction isomorphism on every
two-center overlap, a coefficient group, or functorial composition.
Therefore no sheaf cohomology or holonomy class is canonically defined by
Wave203 alone.

## Gain-graph language needs actual gains

A global orientation is not a gain assignment. The exact control has a
global orientation with `b=0`; it neither forces nor forbids nonzero cycle
gain. Any gain obstruction must first derive group-valued transition maps
from the frozen ternary relations.

## Ordinary circuit elimination is too weak

Matroid circuit elimination produces some circuit in a support union with
one common element removed. It does not preserve two prescribed outer
elements, bound the new circuit's weight, or guarantee that it
cross-realizes a new graph nonedge. Strong symmetric elimination requires
the additional no-skew-circuits hypothesis, which is not known here.

## Hilton--Milner is saturated

The exact local `(7,3)` theorem gives the cap 13. The certificate uses an
equality family at every center. A stability theorem that only recovers
the equality template cannot force cross-center orientation gluing.

## Classical fusion-frame positivity is absent

The 99 projectors sum to zero in characteristic three. Real or complex
tight-fusion-frame simplex bounds assume positive semidefinite Hilbert
geometry and a positive frame constant, so their conclusions cannot be
transported coefficientwise.

## Polar cap terminology is inapplicable

The 231 endpoint points include thousands of orthogonal pairs. Bounds for
partial ovoids or polar caps require the relevant no-collinearity/no-shared-
generator hypothesis and do not apply to this selected singular set.

## The hostile control is not an endpoint object

The circulant is visibly not strongly regular, and its declared local
block/fiber types are not derived from adjacency. There are no ternary
columns or rank equations. The control refutes only a local-interface
lemma; it is not positive evidence for Conway 99.
