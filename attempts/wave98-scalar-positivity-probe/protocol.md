# Wave 98 protocol

## Frozen question

Does the exact Wave 86 formal modular pair with short-shell sum 5,868 develop
a negative, nonintegral, or odd nonconstant coefficient between degrees 51
and 1,000?

## Method

1. Import the sealed Wave 86 basis, Fricke map, and first-15 coefficient
   control.
2. Reconstruct generalized Eisenstein coefficients by divisor sieves rather
   than Wave 86's direct divisor loop.
3. Multiply the 16 needed product series by exact integer convolution.
4. Recover both Fricke-paired series from exact rational coordinates.
5. Compare the first 15 coefficients to the sealed control, then test every
   coefficient through degree 1,000 for integrality, evenness away from the
   constant term, and nonnegativity.

## Evidence boundary

- The probe imports discovery definitions and is not independent verification.
- A finite positive prefix is not an all-orders positivity theorem.
- A scalar modular pair is not a lattice theta pair.
- No rank row, graph, or Conway-99 outcome is established.
- The package claim label is `UNKNOWN`.
