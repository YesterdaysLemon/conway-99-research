## Exact reduction

Let `P` be the standard matching matrix on the first fibre, let `Q` and `R`
be the matching matrices on the second and third fibres, and let `F` be the
permutation matrix joining the second and third fibres. Eliminating the first
twelve-point fibre from the cubic-core matrix `3I-A_core` leaves, up to an
irrelevant global sign,

```text
H = [ P+Q       F+3I+P ]
    [ F^T+3I+P   P+R   ].
```

The eliminated block `3I-P` is invertible over `F_7`, with inverse `3I+P`.
Consequently

```text

```

Put

```text

```

If the alternating partition of `(P,Q)` has `e` even parts, then

```text

```

Let the columns of `N` span `ker(A)` and put `D=N^T B`. The Wave 40 boundary
is exactly `rank(D)=e`. For such an `F`, let the columns of `W` span
`ker(D)`. A standard singular Schur-complement congruence gives



Here `A^-` means any solution operator on `im(A)`. Equation (1) is
well-defined: `BW` lies in `im(A)`, and two solutions differ by a vector in
`ker(A)`, which is orthogonal to `BW`.

projection vector `N^T B_(z,y)`. It then:

1. enumerates every dimension-`e` subspace generated by observed projective
