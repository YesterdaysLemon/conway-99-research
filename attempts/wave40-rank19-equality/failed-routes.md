# Failed and bounded routes

## The 36 rank-19 column-space survivors do not form a graph

For a `2+2+2` block, 36 of the 4,500 count-compatible outside columns lie
in the local column space. All 36 have `a_z=0`, so the twelve mandatory
neighbors of `z` already contradict rank 19. Treating the 36 as candidate
vertices would ignore the forced degree of `z` and is invalid.

## The square-zero identity was not needed

The frozen matrix satisfies `K^2=0`, but attempts to use only the local
`LL` block of that equation introduced the unknown aggregate `C C^T`.
The rank-completion quotient gives a sharper obstruction without guessing
or solving those aggregates. No new claim is made from `K^2=0`.

## Visible common-neighbor filtering is incomplete

Of the 4,356 `a_z=0` patterns, 4,116 pass the necessary condition that a
known adjacent pair does not already have more than one common neighbor
inside `L`. Passing this cap does not prove that a pattern extends to the
full graph. The proof uses it only as a diagnostic and relies on the
overinclusive complete universe for all impossibility statements.

## Rank 22 survives the local quotient test

For a `2+2+2` block and global rank 22, the syndrome space may have
dimension three. Among the 25,744 three-spaces generated by the 144
`Z`-pattern syndromes, exactly 32 support a perfect matching of size twelve.
The package records a canonical witness. Therefore the present argument
cannot be promoted to `r7>=23`.

Extending the route would require coupling the other sixty outside vertices,
the `K^2=0` aggregate, or overlapping edge-local blocks. Absence of such a
coupling is not evidence that rank 22 or the endpoint exists.

## No endpoint or novelty promotion

The conditional floor `r7>=22` remains far below the rational ceiling 44.
It neither excludes `n3=4158` nor improves the general upper bound. No
literature-priority search was performed in this proof lane.
