# Retained failed and null routes

## Type-one packet owners

An attempted proof tried to show that every type-one-oriented exact-one
owner equals the canonical all-equal `3+6` leaf word.  The proposed
two-stage subtraction can return the already selected owner after removing
the exact-three raw and its companion: the translated word differs from
the owner by a full star, and that center-star direction lies in the span
of the companion pair.  Subtracting the companion can also reintroduce a
coordinate omitted by the first translation.

No canonical-leaf conclusion is used in the successful proof.

## Quotient and mod-three identities

At the all-type-three endpoint, the five circuits of one packet have one
class modulo the point-star module, and the 4,158 canonical leaf words sum
to zero over `F_3`.  Both identities are compatible with the known
relation-space dimensions.

## Canonical-pair weight-ten relations

At that same endpoint, leaf words paired by the canonical nonedge
involution differ in a weight-ten relation after their four common square
coordinates cancel.  A proper circuit inside such a relation need not
cross-realize a nonedge, so this does not make the Wave194 bound strict.

## Pairwise leaf differences

Within one packet, two leaf words differ by an adjacent outer-star
relation.  Circuit extraction therefore first returns the already known
edge sector.
