# Boundaries and redirected routes

## Scalar block-intersection profile

Cube and Wagner point sets both meet 12 triangle blocks twice and 32 once.
Any argument using only this histogram cannot distinguish them.

The determinant energy is retained precisely because it depends on how those
incidences fit together.

## Spectrum alone

The spectra of `B*B^T`, the Levi graph, and the block-intersection graph are
forced and consistent. Their positivity does not contradict existence.

## Neighborhood-triple code Gram

The exact Gram matrix `816I+10K` is positive definite over the reals and
self-orthogonal modulo two. Neither fact alone is an obstruction. A useful
continuation must exploit column multiplicities or forbidden local
configurations, not just the row Gram.

## Required next theorem

Classify enough nonzero `8 x 8` incidence minors to express or bound the
contribution in the global energy identity under the prism-free block
codegree condition. Any truncation must state which partial-linear
configurations it omits.
