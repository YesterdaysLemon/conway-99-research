# Failed routes and roadblocks

## One-point invariant projector SDP

The six exact scaffold blocks are feasible for every `y=0,...,42`, and in
fact for every real `y` in the interval. Thus no rational dual certificate
in this linear one-point cone can exclude the endpoint.

## Variance shortcut

The operator-convex inequality

```text
average(B^2) - average(B)^2 >= 0
```

has equality on the fixed 6- and 7-dimensional scaffold subspaces. It
recovers the known Wave 3 edge-orbit relation but leaves strictly positive
slack on the remaining three blocks. It supplies no new upper bound.

## Bounded Schur powers

All 23,388 exact endpoint block inequalities in the declared degree-24
Hadamard family are nonnegative. This is a complete nonhit only for that
family, not for all flag-algebra or Terwilliger certificates.

## Unsafe symmetry shortcut

Fixing a particular orbit-2 matching, orbit-4 cycle, or representative
completion would assume that rooted scaffold symmetry survives in the
unknown graph. This is forbidden. The group may average certificates, but
it may not reduce candidates unless the reduction is proved orbit-complete.

## Fake joint moments

Products of the five averaged edge-orbit counts are not counts of compatible
two-root or triangle-root configurations. The missing correlations must be
represented explicitly before a larger SDP can be sound.
