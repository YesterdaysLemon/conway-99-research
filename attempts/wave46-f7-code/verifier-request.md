Frozen scope: the characteristic-seven row-code consequences of the
conditional `n3=4158` endpoint for the correct 231 by 231 projector
`M=21E_0`.

1. From `M^2=21M`, symmetry, zero row sums, diagonal four, and endpoint
   off-diagonal alphabet `0,+1,-1`, derive that
   `C=row_F7(M)` is self-orthogonal, lies in `1^perp`, has projective
   generator columns, and therefore has `d(C^perp)>=3`.
2. From row composition `4^1,1^32,(-1)^36,0^162`, prove that the six scalar
   compositions are distinct and force `A69>=1386`.
3. Independently enumerate the weights compatible with coordinate sum and
   squared norm zero and confirm that exactly `1,2,4` are excluded.
4. Recompute every degree-zero, degree-one, and degree-two complete
   enumerator moment at the worst rank `r7=28`; confirm strict slack or
   produce a failing symbol or symbol pair.
5. Rebuild, without importing discovery functions, each generic positive
   control for every dimension `28,...,44` from the archived local columns
   and projection matrix. Check:

   - length and generator rank;
   - all row sums;
   - the complete Gram matrix;
   - nonzero and pairwise nonproportional columns;
   - local weight enumerator and the inherited weight-69 count;
   - projection-matrix and generator-column-stream hashes.

6. Independently derive `M^(o3)=M+4I`, its claimed inverse, full Schur-cube
   rank, and the resulting floor `r7>=11`.
7. Confirm that the `A+3I` calculation is a different 99 by 99 object,
   is correct as a hostile control, and is unused by the live conclusion.

## Required separation judgments

- Generic controls do satisfy the stated ordinary code constraints.
- Generic controls are not endpoint projector candidates and do not purport
  to realize the six endpoint generator compositions.
- Passing moments through degree two does not establish a complete weight
  enumerator.
- The discovery package does not exclude `n3=4158`, improve the global upper
  bound, construct a Conway graph, or establish novelty.
