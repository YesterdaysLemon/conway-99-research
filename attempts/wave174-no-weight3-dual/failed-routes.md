# Boundary and failed shortcuts

## Degree-four positivity of the Wave 173 rational control

The six-cell rational control from Wave 173 has positive complete dual
coefficients in total degrees four, five, and six.  Those coefficients are
nonintegral, so the control is not a code, but degree-four nonnegativity is
not an obstruction.

## Pairwise `C=+2` is not enough

Three pairwise `C=+2` block relations only give an induced `3K3`.  Wave 172
correctly warned that pairwise orthogonality does not imply row dependence.
Wave 174 starts from an actual weight-three dependence and uses its
point--triangle line-sum equation.

## The first quotient does not eliminate every local parameter

Color-class interlacing alone leaves several `(c,t)` pairs.  The decisive
extra inputs are pointwise equality of the two cross-color degrees,
five-cell interlacing, and the exact common-neighbor identity for `Z3`.

## No endpoint promotion

Dual distance four is compatible with many abstract ternary codes and caps
in projective space.  A further argument must retain the symmetric Gram
matrix, its 231 distinguished isotropic columns, and the 99 point-star
relations.  No endpoint contradiction is claimed here.
