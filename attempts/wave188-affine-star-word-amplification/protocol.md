# Protocol

1. Validate every direct frozen input manifest.
2. Work only in the conditional prism-free rank-11 endpoint model.
3. Count translated dual relation words directly; do not replace them by
   circuits or infer that distinct supports extract distinct circuits.
4. Prove projective distinctness in the quotient by the two full-star
   relations.
5. Separate the one-block-side projector case from the at-least-two-block
   support-capacity case.
6. Use private labels only to prove outside-cover status and to prevent
   cross-type collisions.
7. Keep the result `DERIVED` until a clean-room verifier reconstructs every
   orbit, capacity, and counting step without importing discovery code.
8. Do not promote the word lower bound to a rank-11 contradiction without
   an independently verified incompatible upper bound.
