# Failed and bounded routes

## Circuit extraction is unnecessary and weaker

Distinct translated relation supports can contain the same smaller circuit.
Therefore their affine-orbit count must not be promoted to a circuit count.
Wave 188 counts the actual dual words, which are already the objects in
`B_4+...+B_9`.

## Generic capacity three loses the new information

Applying the Wave-179 ceiling of three to every translated word recovers
only a weaker incidence bound. The support split matters: a word with at
least two blocks on each side has capacity one above weight four and
capacity at most two at weight four.

## One-block sides need projector classification

A one-block side cannot simply be called a singleton. It expresses a
singular column in the opposite six-space. The one-adjacency case is
projector-singular, while the anticomplete case is exactly the verified
three-word conic/complement orbit.

## The scalar lower bound is not a code obstruction

Known ordinary MacWilliams controls permit more than 18,018 low-weight dual
words. A contradiction still needs marked complete-weight information or a
new upper bound consuming the singular quadric, zero frame, and 99 star
spaces simultaneously.
