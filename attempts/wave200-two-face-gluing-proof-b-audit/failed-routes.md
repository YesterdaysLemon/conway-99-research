# Retained boundaries and attacked routes

## Non-additive interpretation

Applying a four-unit loss separately to each saturated label is invalid
unless overlap is excluded. The repair is structural: fixed-center leaf
labels are partitioned by their unique pair type, and a saturated label
exhausts all five flags of that type. Distinct saturated labels therefore
have distinct fibers, so the losses really do add.
