# Failed routes and retained hostile boundaries

## 1. Category-summed integer lift

Summing `Az=4x-z+2t*1` over `P` or `N` reproduces the signed first/second
moments already sealed in Wave 207.  This package does not reuse those sums
as new evidence.

## 2. Spectral norms alone

For balanced weight 14, the exact splitting leaves six nonnegative norm
rows `q.q=0,14,28,42,56,70`.  Positivity and integrality alone exclude none
of them.  In particular, `q=0` retains a graphical degree sequence with one
cross edge; the package does not claim that sequence extends.

## 3. Complementary-Fano token capacity

The complete seven-point capacity census removes same-sign overlap
`alpha>=2`, and a coupled endpoint argument removes `alpha=1`.  It leaves
651 labelled `alpha=0` capacity rows.  A capacity survivor is only a
necessary incidence assignment, not a codeword.

## 4. Twenty-two-vertex hostile control

An exact partial graph realizes the `alpha=0,k=3` lift equations and all
displayed `lambda/mu` upper caps.  It deliberately omits 77 vertices,
degree completion, common-neighbor lower equalities, and equations at the
omitted vertices.  It blocks promotion of the local reduction to a
weight-14 exclusion.

## 5. Residue shell quantization

The identity

```text
Q.Q=99t(9-t)+162L
```

is exact, but every spectral candidate retained for weights 17, 20, and 23
lands in an allowed shell.  Treating unknown shell coefficients as zero
would be invalid.  No residual weight is excluded here.
