## Local discriminant groups do not exclude a row

The `2,3,5` primary groups and their Gauss phases are fixed independently
of `k`.  Milgram forces both elementary seven-primary groups to be
`O^-(k,7)`.  Every imported even value `16<=k<=30` is compatible with that
type.

## Rootlessness is not enough

Both positive-definite eigenlattices have minimum at least four, but their
determinants grow by `7^k`.  Elementary determinant-density estimates have
ample slack and do not exclude a row.

## Gauss phase is not complete local classification

The calculation fixes the primary groups, all primary Gauss phases, and the
seven-primary determinant type.  It does not yet give a canonical Jordan
symbol for the two-adic refinements.  A finer two-adic classification could
be useful only if combined with an independent theorem classifying
rootless lattices of the displayed ranks and discriminant forms.  Treating
`W=im(Q)` as an even lattice would still be invalid; the ambient
characteristic correction in the quadratic formula is essential.
