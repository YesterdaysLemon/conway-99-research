# Wave 185 boundaries and failed continuations

## The transition totals are not an equitable quotient

The table fixes totals over the ten incident and ten orthogonal local-root
cells.  It does not make the individual cell-pair counts constant.  Dividing
the totals by ten would assume an automorphism that has not been proved.

## The pair capacity is six

For an incident cell pair, each of the two orientations gives a `K_{2,2}`.
The common-neighbor equations forbid four selected edges but allow the
immediate three-edge path pattern.  The proved combined cap is six, not four.

## The type-five frame is not yet contradictory

The identity `sum r tensor r=0` makes the type-five root code
self-orthogonal, but ordinary dimension and projective-length bounds allow
the current lengths, including the arithmetic lower profile `n_5=15`.

## Arithmetic profiles are not constructions

The parameter pairs in the exact results satisfy only the scalar incidence
equation and new lower bound.  They do not realize the rooted transitions,
support intersections, local `A6` systems, frame identity, or graph.
