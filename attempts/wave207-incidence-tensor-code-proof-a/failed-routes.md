# Failed routes and retained gaps

## 1. The Wave 206 relaxed weight-eight control is not incidence-linear

The earlier eight-column control verifies the quadratic tensor equation,
cap condition, singularity, and an ambient rank-11 embedding.  It does not
verify `a in im(B^T)`.  In particular, its displayed signed first moment is
nonzero.  It therefore cannot refute the new identity `Za=0` or the
concurrent-secant theorem.

## 2. Concurrency at an external point does not violate the cap condition

The cap condition forbids three *selected* columns on one projective line.
The common point of the four secants is external to the selected 231-point
set.  Four concurrent secants are therefore compatible with dual distance
four.  Treating concurrency itself as a contradiction would be invalid.

## 3. Internal four-circuits are not automatically incidence words

The canonical support contains twelve projective weight-four circuits and
eight projective weight-five circuits.  These are true relations among the
centered columns, but the derivation does not put each smaller relation in
`im(B^T)`.  Nor does it show that their triangle supports cross-realize any
particular graph-vertex pair.  The Wave 177--179 transversal bounds cannot
be imported without this missing incidence statement.

## 4. The polar restriction patterns are necessary, not impossible

Rank zero, two, three, and four restrictions force respectively `K8`,
`2K4`, `4K2`, and `2C4` anticompleteness graphs on the eight triangle
blocks.  Existing SRG and prism-free identities do not exclude all of
these patterns.  A restricted nonhit or an intuition that many mutually
anticomplete triangles are unlikely would not prove nonexistence.

## 5. A nonzero Gram entry does not identify block intersection

At the endpoint, Gram value two certifies two cross edges between disjoint
blocks, and Gram value zero certifies no cross edge.  Gram value one can
mean either intersecting blocks or disjoint blocks with one cross edge.
The canonical polar matrix therefore does not determine the eight-block
triangle-incidence hypergraph.

## 6. The `M_7g` classification is not an endpoint construction

Kaipa--Pradhan classify the ambient finite-geometric support orbit.  Their
configuration supplies neither the remaining 223 selected columns nor the
99-by-231 incidence, star projectors, SRG, or prism-free graph.  Conversely,
the classification does not exclude an endpoint realization.

## 7. Remaining exact bridge

The unresolved question is now sharply finite and incidence-specific:

```text
Can one of the four signed M_7g polar patterns occur as a word of
im(B^T) for the actual degree-(7,3) triangle incidence while all 99 star
relations and the full 231-column cap are retained?
```

An answer needs a symbolic cocircuit/line-sum theorem or a simultaneous
star restriction.  Broad enumeration of abstract eight-point caps would
only rediscover the already classified `M_7g` orbit and would not close
this bridge.
