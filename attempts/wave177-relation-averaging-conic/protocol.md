# Protocol

1. Consume Wave 176 only at its independently verified conditional scope.
2. Average over true coefficient-relation codes, not Gram kernels.
3. Remove the complete individual-star subcode before calling a word
   genuinely cross-star.
4. Use full support only after exhibiting a subcode word active in every
   coordinate.
5. Apply dual distance four only as the lower support bound.
6. Treat one short word per pair as indexed, not necessarily distinct.
7. Use all 13 points of `PG(2,3)` only as a local divisibility control, not
   as a candidate cap or endpoint graph.
8. Perform no graph, code, SAT, or isomorphism search.
9. Require independent verification before promotion.
