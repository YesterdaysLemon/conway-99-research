# Boundary and failed shortcuts

## Average weight is existence, not multiplicity

The exact averages `8` and `28/3` prove at least one short genuine
cross-star word per vertex pair.  They do not prove that choices from
different pairs are distinct.  Counting 693 edges as 693 distinct dual
words would be invalid.

## A weight-four relation is not itself a contradiction

The type `4+2` word is a complete plane conic.  Its outer-product sum is
`-I`, while the nine remaining points of `PG(2,3)` contribute `+I`.
Projective 3-divisibility and the zero-frame identity therefore admit an
exact local completion.

## Circuit elimination needs labelled overlap data

Matroid circuit elimination can create another circuit only when two
chosen relations share a supported block with compatible coefficients.
The present pairwise theorem does not control those overlaps.  A valid
continuation must derive global multiplicity or incidence constraints
before applying elimination.
