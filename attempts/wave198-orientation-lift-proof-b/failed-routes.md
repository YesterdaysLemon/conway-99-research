# Retained failed and null routes

## Unordered degree-ten saturation

The Wave197 row treats a label of selected multiplicity ten as one element
of the oriented union.  Such a label necessarily occupies both
orientations, since either orientation has capacity five.  The missing
orientation is exactly what the `S5` row restores.

## Rank-three association-scheme moments

The ordinary first and second moments of a saturated 39-vertex leaf union
do not by themselves obstruct gluing; they admit a nonempty interval of
internal edge counts.  The successful invariant is the finer orientation
incidence, not the unrefined SRG subset moment.

## Remaining rational null

The new exact certificate has a nonnegative rational active row.  It is
not an endpoint object, but it means the displayed linear rows alone do
not yield a contradiction.
