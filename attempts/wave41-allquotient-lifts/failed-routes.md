# Wave 41 failed routes and boundaries

## 1. No rank-eleven quotient has an empty lift space

All eight normalized rank-eleven records have 37,378 triangle-free
endpoint-pairing masks. Therefore triangle-freeness of the 36-vertex core
does not exclude any record.

## 2. The local rank does not reach the global ceiling

Every rank-eleven record has 264 lifts with `rank_F7(K39)=33`. The maximum
seen is 35, not 45. The one-triangle characteristic-seven rank route alone
does not contradict the full rank ceiling 44.

## 3. Normalized multiplicity was not structural diversity

The eight records form one exact fibre-coloured quotient isomorphism class.
This strengthens the local theorem, but it cannot be multiplied into eight
independent global cases. The normalization records labelled forms, not
pairwise nonisomorphic quotients.

## 4. The full 4,050-form triangle-free count gives no exclusion

Every normalized all-`222` quotient admits at least 10,648 triangle-free
pairing masks. Nineteen forms admit all 262,144 masks. Absence of a local
triangle is therefore much too weak to force a triangular prism globally.

## 5. Affine mask transport is not a graph construction

The exact affine maps classify pairing masks under quotient relabelling and
endpoint swaps. They do not impose the simultaneous 60-column incidence
matrix, the outside graph, or consistency among overlapping base triangles.

## 6. The naive rank-33 border argument is automatically vacuous

At rank 33 the local kernel has dimension six. A full rank at most 44 would
require the kernel-to-outside border rank to be at most five. But every
`K39` already has three independent fibre-star kernel vectors that
annihilate every balanced outside column:

```text
(4+1+1) + (10-2) = 14 = 0 mod 7.
```

Therefore the border rank is automatically at most three, even stronger
than the required ceiling. The symmetric border lemma cannot force rank 45.
Any continuation must use the simultaneous binary design, outside graph,
`K^2=0`, or overlap constraints rather than raw border rank.
