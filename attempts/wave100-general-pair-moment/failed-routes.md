# Wave 100 boundary

The improved slope is a global bridge but not a strict upper bound on `n3`.
Wave 86 constrains the sum of three short shells only in the rank-28 row;
norm-16 and norm-18 vectors can still absorb the required count.

The next needed theorem is a compatible upper bound on those two shells or
a direct incompatibility between their labelled support systems and the
rooted transitions.
