# Failed and redirected routes

## Order-eleven two-completion moment

Status: `UNNECESSARY_FOR_FIRST_MOMENT`.

The completion is unique when it exists. A success is already an induced
order-eight event. Higher order may still be useful for overlapping marks,
but not to resolve multiplicity of one mark.

## Marginal left/right candidate counts

Status: `INSUFFICIENT`.

Marginals do not determine pairings. Cauchy-Schwarz gives the wrong
direction for the required failure upper bound.

## Pairwise incompatibility graph

Status: `REFUTED_FROM_CURRENT_CLAUSES`.

Five optional failures coexist in an explicit degree-saturated local gadget.
No Hoffman bound or clique cover whose edges use only the currently encoded
pairwise/root-local clauses can prove independence number at most four.

## Retained route

Use a failure hypergraph, lifted moment matrix, or full exact conic dual that
contains global extension compatibility. A scalar collection of sampled
four-root cuts is not a substitute for the whole root-3/root-12 PSD blocks.
