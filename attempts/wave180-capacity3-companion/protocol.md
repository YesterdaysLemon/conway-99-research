# Protocol

1. Freeze the independently verified Wave 174 dual-distance theorem, Wave
   176 star projectors, and Wave 179 exact-transversal multiplicity theorem.
2. Restrict the equality analysis to circuit supports serving three
   **nonedge** labels; supports serving only two nonedges remain in the
   multiplicity-two part of the cover.
3. Derive the seven `j(T,S_i)` counts from `lambda=1` and `mu=2`, without
   assuming an automorphism or enumerating a graph.
4. Use the projector only after the circuit relation proves `z_T in E_x`.
5. Distinguish Gram-kernel relations from true column relations; here the
   eight columns lie in the nondegenerate six-space `E_x`, so the two
   kernels agree.
6. Retain both normalized circuit relations: weight four on the marked
   triple and weight five on its four-block complement.
7. Choose an inclusion-minimal nonedge cover before using the companion
   involution.
8. Count projective circuit supports before applying the two nonzero
   ternary scalars.
9. Perform no graph, code, SAT, configuration, or isomorphism search.
10. Require a clean-room verifier to reproduce the local classification,
    companion minimality, and global cover inequality before promotion.
