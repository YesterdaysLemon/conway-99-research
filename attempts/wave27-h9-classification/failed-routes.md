# Wave 27 failed routes and scope boundary

## Determinant or minimum forces `A2`

Refuted.  `E8^4 orthogonal_sum E6^2` is even, positive definite, rank 44,
determinant nine, and has no orthogonal `A2` root component.  Its scaled
dual `21S^(-1)` is even integral with minimum 28.

## Discriminant form forces `A2^2`

Refuted.  `E6^2` and `A2^2` have isometric order-nine discriminant
quadratic modules.  The exact change of generators is
`[[1,1],[1,-1]]` modulo three.

## Root-basis-only `E6` trace floor 18

Refuted as an unrestricted claim.  Restricting `Q6` to a Gram matrix of an
`E6` root basis yields trace 18 in the tested parity class, but the exact
non-root-basis form in the report has trace 14.  Any search retaining the
root-basis restriction must label it explicitly.

## First rank-one vector convention

The vector `(-1,-1,0,1,0,0)` was tested against the report's `E6` Cartan
ordering and produced fifth-denominators in `I+8P` and `H(I+8P)`.  It
belongs to a different coordinate convention and is not evidence here.
The corrected vector for the frozen Cartan matrix is
`(-1,0,1,0,0,-1)`; all claimed matrices are regenerated from it.

## Theta/modular-form uniqueness

Not obtained.  The two exact determinant-nine controls already have
different norm-two coefficients, 1104 and 1212, despite isometric
discriminant modules.  No modular-form argument was found that selects one
coefficient from the inherited arithmetic premises.

## Genus/mass classification

Not performed.  No claim is made that the two displayed lattices exhaust a
genus or all rank-44 determinant-nine classes.  A complete neighbor/mass

## Full projector or Schur-square realization

Not obtained.  The package supplies no 231-row integral frame, no Gram
matrix with the required entry histogram, no cubic tensor certificate, and
no graph.  The Wave 26 obstruction is avoided only because `S` has no
orthogonal `A2` summand; avoidance is not realization.

## Status wall

exclude `n3=708`, construct Conway-99, classify all `h=9` forms, or
establish novelty.
