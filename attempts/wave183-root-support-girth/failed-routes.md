# Boundary and failed continuations

## The all-nine spectrum is superseded

Wave 182 showed that an all-nine support design has a positive semidefinite
incidence Gram.  The present argument excludes every individual
multiplicity-nine support before that spectral boundary is reached.  The
old positive spectrum was a valid null test, but is no longer the sharp
boundary.

## Support existence is not simultaneous gluing

The abstract graphs `5K1`, `3K2`, and `C7` exist.  Their existence does not
construct the required root labels, local `A6` systems, canonical
quadrilateral involution, or target graph.

## The first split frame has no rank separation yet

The identity

```text
sum_(m=7) r tensor r=sum_(m=5) r tensor r
```

would be contradictory if one side were forced to have larger rank than the
other.  The current incidence counts do not supply such a rank inequality.

## The root-count improvement is conditional

The interval `297..415` is proved only on the Wave 181 equality face
`Q=2079`.  It is not a new unconditional circuit-enumerator inequality and
does not by itself make the Wave 180 lower bound strict.

## Remaining analytic bridge

A continuation should combine the three support shapes with local `A6`
orthogonality, the opposite-diagonal involution, or the split tensor frame.
Repeating the ordinary incidence spectrum or enumerating small regular
graphs would not address simultaneous global compatibility.
