# Failed and bounded routes

## Literal discriminant enumeration

The two groups have sizes `7^14` and `7^30`. Literal class enumeration is
unnecessary and was not attempted. Exact quadratic-value orbit sums reduce
the relevant information to eight aggregates in each group.

## Treating orthogonal orbits as automorphism orbits

Rejected. A finite discriminant-form isometry need not lift to a lattice
automorphism or a graph automorphism. The calculation sums components over
complete exact-value sets; it never asserts componentwise equality.

## Projective square/nonsquare collapse

Rejected. Wave 97 showed that projectivization already merges short-vector
branches. The model preserves each exact value in `F7` and records the
Wave 80 norm-14/16/18 symbol compositions separately.

## Expecting all eight aggregates to add new inequalities

This does not happen. Six anisotropic aggregates are residue dissections of
ordinary scalar theta coefficients. The genuinely new inequalities are
only the two nonzero-isotropic differences

```text
x_(7m)-y_m >= 0
y_(7n)-x_n >= 0.
```

## Raising the Wave 101 norm-20 or norm-22 optima

Null. The exact Wave 101 primal optimizers remain feasible after every
orbit inequality and after extending `Theta_L` positivity through `y77`.
The old exact dual certificates therefore stay sharp.

## Rounding the conditional norm-20/22 rational optimum

Not done. The exact control attaining

```text
x10+x11=4144144
```

has fractional `y` coefficients with denominators 7 and 49. Without a
combined congruence-lattice certificate, parity rounding would be status
inflation.

## Abstract codeword-count upper bound

The exact upper

```text
7^30 (7^13+7^6)
```

for each anisotropic value is much too large to meet the conditional lower
bounds. Coordinate composition or marked-frame constraints are the missing
ingredient.

## Keeping the Wave 116 index-630 marking

Unnecessary. The alternating combination of the unscaled `u_i` already
lies in `M subset L`. Its K marking has index 70 and its L partner index
10, a factor-nine reduction.

## Jacobi support as the missing C4 cap

Null. The target `r=28` satisfies the index-70 support condition at
`q^7,q^8,q^9,q^10`. An explicit symmetric nonnegative coefficient table
already carries all 52,812 target incidences. Modular coupling among the
20 theta components is essential.

## Claiming a complete 20-component Jacobi LP

Not done. The exact theta decomposition and finite Fourier transformation
are derived, but a spanning weight-43/2 K/L module, a truncation theorem,
and an exact upper dual are not yet present.
