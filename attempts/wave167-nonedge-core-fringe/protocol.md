assumption `P=0`.

## Required checks

1. Derive the 22-edge cross graph without assuming automorphisms.
2. Prove all four apex-apex cross edges are absent, separating `lambda=1`
   exclusions from `P=0` exclusions.
3. Reconstruct the 18-core/four-fringe split by lane
   inclusion-exclusion.
4. Check each fringe failure by exact support, not by a diagram.
5. Prove completion uniqueness without confusing it with existence.
6. Audit all incidence multiplicities in `F=166320-8*W8`.
7. Separate the pointwise target from the weaker global defect target.
8. Retain an explicit shell showing which local inference fails.

## Promotion rules

- The discovery derivation cannot certify itself.
- The abstract shell is not an SRG candidate or completion.
- The endpoint stays `UNKNOWN` unless a checked global certificate excludes
  it.
- No literature novelty is inferred from an internal derivation.
- No computation may start below the user's 18 percent launch threshold, and
  it must stop before 15 percent free memory.
