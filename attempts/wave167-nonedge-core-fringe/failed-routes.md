# Failed and redirected routes

## Pointwise four-failure lemma from one nonedge shell

Status: `REFUTED_AS_A_LOCAL_CONSEQUENCE`.

The one-root degree equations and root-visible prism exclusions prove four
mandatory fringe failures. They do not prove that all 36 core marks succeed.
The explicit abstract shell in `derivation.md` retains the equations while
creating additional impure core marks.

This is not a counterexample in a full strongly regular graph. It identifies
the missing information as cross-root or higher-order compatibility.

## Completion uniqueness as completion existence

Status: `INVALID_INFERENCE`.

The `mu=2` condition makes a completion vertex unique if a valid completion
exists. It does not force the unique candidate to have the required
singleton support or the remaining Wagner adjacency.

## Pair-root order-eight moment cone

Status: `INSUFFICIENT`.

The exact Wave 150 pseudowitness survives the pair-root centered PSD
constraints and the two scalar cube/Wagner bounds. Wave 159 further shows
that a finite collection of sampled four-root cuts can remain exactly
feasible while full root-3 and root-12 blocks still expose negative
directions. Sampled cuts cannot certify the required global defect bound.
