# Wave 120 protocol

Date frozen: 2026-07-28.

Role: proof A / alternative-space discovery.

## Frozen scope

Assume a hypothetical `srg(99,14,1,2)` and fix one labelled induced
four-cycle.  Choose the unique representative of each antipodal extension
whose four coordinates are

```text
s = (1,-1,1,-1).
```

Study pairs of norm-16, norm-18, and norm-20 integer `-4` eigenvectors
with this common orientation.  No graph automorphism or cycle stabilizer
is assumed.

The norm-14/16/18 integer profiles and the fixed-C4 partition are imported
from independently verified packages.  The norm-20 unit profile is imported
from sealed Wave 96 discovery and remains conditional on independent
verification.

## Questions

1. What is the exact real affine minimum for the doubled coordinates `2s`?
2. How far do integrality and the four anchor equations sharpen it?
3. What pairwise inner products survive the sum/difference norm dictionary?
4. Can an exact PSD, Gram, spherical Delsarte, or binary minimum-distance
   relaxation force the desired extension caps 25 or 24?

## Gates

- A formal Gram or code witness is not an eigenvector family.
- Low-norm magnitude profiles do not certify the required induced support
  graph.
- A lower bound sharp for the four anchor equations is not called attained
  in the full graph.
- Failure of pairwise relaxations does not refute higher-order, parity-dual,
  or simultaneous outside-adjacency arguments.
- Discovery does not verify itself.
