# Focused source audit

## Makhnev--Nirova (2006)

A. A. Makhnev and M. S. Nirova, *Slender partial quadrangles and their
automorphisms*, Algebra and Logic 45 (2006), 344--352.

- Primary record:
  <https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/al161>
- DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10469-006-0031-6>
- Accessible primary PDF:
  <https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=al&paperid=161&what=fullt>

The abstract's phrase "classification for partial quadrangles with
`t<=6`" must not be read as an existence classification.  Theorem 1 lists
the possible collinearity graphs.  Its item (3) explicitly includes a graph
with parameters `(99,14,1,2)`.  Lemma 2.4 reaches the target at
`m=4,t=6,s=2,mu=2`.  The paper therefore narrows the parameter possibilities
but neither constructs nor excludes the target.

## Pech (2021)

Christian Pech, *On highly regular strongly regular graphs*, Algebraic
Combinatorics 4 (2021), 843--878.

- Article and primary PDF:
  <https://doi.org/10.5802/alco.183>
  and <https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/alco.183.pdf>

Theorem 5.5 recalls the characterization of partial-quadrangle point graphs.
Theorem 5.7 proves that every such point graph satisfies the 5-vertex
condition.  The later 3-isoregularity results require uniform triad-center
data or special generalized-quadrangle hypotheses; they do not promote
`PQ(2,6,2)` to a 3-isoregular geometry.

Theorem 5.10 does not yield a hidden parameter obstruction.  Substitution of
`(s,t,mu)=(2,6,2)` gives its necessary inequality as

```text
710>=200,
```

strictly.  Equality would require every triad to have one center, whereas the
parameter-forced distribution has 27,720 one-center and 70,686 zero-center
triads.  Proposition 5.14 assumes the missing uniformity rather than proving
it.  Thus the target lies outside the 3-isoregular regime but violates no
condition quoted from this paper.

in their stated scope.  This is source evidence, not a mathematical proof
