# Failed and exhausted routes

Claim label: `CANDIDATE`.

## Symmetric-square dimension

The entrywise square of \(S\bmod7\) is \(J-I\), whose rank is 98. Factoring
\(S\bmod7\) through an \(r\)-dimensional bilinear space therefore gives
\(r(r+1)/2\ge98\), or \(r\ge14\).

This is exact but weaker than the independently verified \(r\ge28\). The
nilpotence relation supplies exactly the one visible dependency among the 99
pure squares, so this dimension count cannot be tightened without adding
higher-order graph information.

## Smith-profile contradiction

The local identities determine the entire Smith form in terms of the one
unknown \(r=\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbf F_7}(S)\). However, each
\(r=28,\ldots,44\) gives a valid divisibility chain, determinant, and local
rank profile. Integral invariant factors alone therefore do not contradict the
endpoint.

## What would be needed next

A stronger continuation must restrict which of these abstract Smith profiles
can be realized by a symmetric zero-diagonal \(\{\pm1\}\)-matrix satisfying
\(S^2=49(I+J)\). Candidate additions include:

- higher symmetric powers with exact relations inherited from the graph;
- compatibility between the 7-adic lattice filtration and individual
  \(\{\pm1\}\) coordinates;
- local subconfiguration data coupled to the global Smith filtration.

Merely recomputing determinant, modular ranks, or the square-zero Jordan type
will reproduce the constraints already exhausted here.
