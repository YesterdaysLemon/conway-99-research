# Failed routes and quarantined shortcuts

## Smith form alone

The Smith form fixes valuations and cyclic-factor orders, not the odd
determinant unit of the even unimodular `3`-eigenlattice. Exact opposite-sign
controls share the same two-primary Smith factors.

## Discriminant-group order

```text

```

discriminant form is invalid.

## Global Milgram phase

The signature fixes the product of all local Gauss phases. It does not
isolate the required two-primary component without the odd-primary forms
and exact normalization conventions.

## Self-duality and Type II shortcuts

The adjacency row lattice is even but not asserted unimodular or
self-dual. The binary image code is LCD, not self-dual. No Type II
classification theorem applies as stated.

## Local controls as graphs

The controls prove insufficiency of binary projection, spectral, Smith,
and group-level data. They are not integral zero-one 14-regular adjacency
matrices. They cannot be used to claim that the full SRG identities admit
both signs.
