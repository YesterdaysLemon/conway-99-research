# Wave 80 retained null routes

## Orthogonal type alone

At \(q=16\), the quotient is \(O^-(16,7)\), not an arbitrary nondegenerate
space.  This does not exclude the Wave 71 alternatives: its seven
hyperbolic planes already represent zero and every nonzero element of
\(\mathbf F_7\).  Self-dots \(0,4,1\) all survive.

## Norm 14 and the hull

A norm-14 vector gives an isotropic codeword.  Its class may be zero, meaning
the word lies in the Seidel hull, or nonzero isotropic in \(C/R\).  The
current data exclude neither possibility.  In particular, isotropic does
not imply radical.

## Ordinary MacWilliams moments

The exact dual-distance improvement gives orthogonal-array moments through
degree five.  The 594 mandatory weight-98 Seidel words and a minimally
forced six-word short scalar line leave enormous positive slack in every
one.  No complete integral enumerator was constructed, so the result is
only a scoped null obstruction.

## Binary-shadow analogy

There is no canonical binary Type-I shadow in this odd-characteristic,
non-self-dual setting.  The exact replacement is the discriminant
orthogonal decomposition
\[
O^-(42,7)=O^-(16,7)\perp O^+(26,7)
\]
at rank 28.  Rebranding that decomposition as a shadow adds no constraint.

## Support six

An exact locally admissible singular six-vertex Seidel block with a
full-support kernel and 12 compatible one-vertex outside incidence patterns
is retained as a positive control.  It is not a global graph fragment
construction, but it shows that the support-five argument does not extend
unchanged.  Wave 80 therefore stops at the proved bound
\(d(C^\perp)\ge6\); it makes no weight-six exclusion.
